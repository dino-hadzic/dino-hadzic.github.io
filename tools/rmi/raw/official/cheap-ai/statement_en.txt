Romanian Master of Informatics, 28 November 2025, Day 2 — Problem CheapAI
(English rendering of the official Romanian statement cheapai-ro.pdf; no official English PDF exists.)

You are a small, stressed mouse trying to survive the ever faster rise of AI startups. With competitors
popping up everywhere, gaining a technological edge seems almost impossible. In your desperate search for an
advantage you came across CheapAI(TM), a startup with funds so small that even you can afford to work there.
Your first mission is to build the cheapest tokenizer in the world.

You were hired by CheapAI(TM) and your first mission is to build the tokenizer. Unfortunately the budget is very
small, and besides the 26 letters of the English alphabet you can afford only one additional token, of length at
most K.

You are given a string S of lowercase English letters and a number K. Your goal is to choose a token (string) of
length at most K such that, after replacing (non-overlapping) occurrences of the chosen token with the special
character #, the total length of the resulting string is minimal.

PROBLEM
Given a number K and a string S of lowercase English letters, choose a non-empty string (token) t with
1 <= |t| <= K, replace occurrences of t in S (chosen without overlapping) with the special character #, so that
the final string has minimal length. Determine the minimal length.

IMPLEMENTATION
You must implement the function:  int solve(int K, std::string S);
It receives K and S and must return the minimal length of the string obtained after replacing the
(non-overlapping) occurrences of the chosen token of length at most K with the special character #.

CONSTRAINTS
1 <= K <= |S| <= 200 000; S consists of lowercase English letters.

Subtasks (#, points, constraints):
1: 5 points — S_i = 'a' for all 1 <= i <= |S|
2: 7 points — |S| <= 100
3: 12 points — |S| <= 5000
4: 40 points — |S| <= 75 000
5: 36 points — |S| <= 200 000

EXAMPLES (PDF text extraction interleaves the columns; the correct reading is:)
Example 1: Input K=5, S=aabaabacbbaabaa -> Output 7. Explanation: choose t = aabaa; S becomes #bacbb#, of length 7.
Example 2: Input K=8, S=aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa (19 a's) -> Output 4. Explanation: choose t = aaaaaa; S becomes ###a, of length 4.
