## About the contests

### ICPC

**ICPC** (International Collegiate Programming Contest) is organised by the ICPC Foundation and is the most influential collegiate computing competition. Because ACM used to sponsor it, many people are still in the habit of calling it the ACM contest.

ICPC is mainly divided into Regionals and the World Finals.

Official website: <https://icpc.global>

### CCPC

Official website: <https://ccpc.io>

The China Collegiate Programming Contest.

A notable difference from ICPC is that many universities do not reimburse participation costs.

## Contest format

Usually a team of three shares one machine and has multiple submission attempts during the contest. Submissions are judged in real time and the verdict is returned immediately; each wrong submission incurs a 20-minute penalty, so the more wrong attempts, the larger the penalty. A problem only scores once all test cases are correct. The ranking is determined by the number of solved problems, and ties are broken by total time. The total time is the sum of the times of each problem; the time of a problem is the number of minutes from the contest start until it is solved plus the penalties on that problem.

In some ICPC-related contests the scoreboard is frozen one hour before the end; submissions and ranking changes after the freeze are not visible to other contestants.

In ICPC-related contests, contestants are allowed to bring a certain amount of printed material.

Besides ICPC and CCPC, many other contests use this format, such as LeetCode weekly contests and the national programming contest, Nowcoder beginner rounds, practice rounds and challenge rounds.

## Season schedule

-   ICPC/CCPC online contests (late August to early September)
-   ICPC/CCPC regionals (late September to late November)
-   ICPC EC Final/CCPC Final (mid-December)
-   ICPC World Finals (April to June of the following year)

## Training guide

### Multi-University Training

Training contests held in summer on [HDU OJ](http://acm.hdu.edu.cn). They have prizes and high-quality problems, and over the years a rich collection of resources has accumulated.

Keyword to search for on the OJ: `Multi-University Training Contest`.

### Domestic regionals

Curated problem sets can be found on [Virtual Judge](https://vjudge.net/).
