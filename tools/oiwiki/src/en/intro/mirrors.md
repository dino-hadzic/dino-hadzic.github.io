title: Mirror sites

**OI Wiki** is hosted on servers outside China, so for various reasons access may sometimes be slow or unavailable.

A status page has been set up: <https://status.oi-wiki.org>, which monitors whether the **OI Wiki** sites are online. If you cannot access the site, open the status page and look for a mirror you can connect to.
