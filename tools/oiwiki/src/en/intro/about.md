## About the project

**OI Wiki** aims to be a free, open and continuously updated site that gathers knowledge about **competitive programming**.

## How to get in touch

The project mainly communicates through [Issues](https://github.com/OI-wiki/OI-wiki/issues)/[QQ](https://jq.qq.com/?_wv=1027&k=5EfkM6K)/[Telegram](https://t.me/OI_wiki).

The Telegram group is [@OI\_wiki](https://t.me/OI_wiki) and the QQ group number is [588793226](https://jq.qq.com/?_wv=1027&k=5EfkM6K) – everyone is welcome to join.

???+ note "Note"
    In principle these groups are **OI Wiki discussion groups**, so please try not to post too much content unrelated to **OI Wiki** in them.
