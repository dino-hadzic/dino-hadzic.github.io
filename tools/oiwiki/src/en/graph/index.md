---
title: Introduction to graph theory
---

**Graph theory** is a branch of mathematics whose main object of study is the graph. A **graph** is a structure made of a given set of vertices and of edges connecting pairs of vertices; such structures are usually used to describe some specific relation between certain objects. Vertices represent the objects, and an edge connecting two vertices indicates that the two objects are in that relation.
