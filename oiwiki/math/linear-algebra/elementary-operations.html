Elementarne matrice

Sljedeće tri vrste kvadratnih matrica nazivaju se elementarnim matricama.

Matrica množenja

Matrica množenja (scaling matrix) posebna je dijagonalna matrica.

\[ D_i(k)=\operatorname{diag}\{1,\cdots,1,k,1,\cdots,1\} \]

Označava dijagonalnu matricu kojoj je \(i\)-ti element na glavnoj dijagonali jednak \(k\), pri čemu se zahtijeva da \(k\) nije \(0\), a svi ostali elementi jednaki su \(1\).

Posebno, kad je \(k\) jednak \(1\), \(D_i(1)\) upravo je jedinična matrica \(I\).

Matrica zamjene

Matrica zamjene (switching matrix) posebna je simetrična matrica.

\[ P_{ij}=\begin{pmatrix} I_{i-1} & & & & \\ & 0 & & 1 & \\ & & I_{j-i-1} & & \\ & 1 & & 0 & \\ & & & & I_{n-j}\\ \end{pmatrix} \]

Svi elementi matrice zamjene jesu \(1\) ili \(0\); na glavnoj dijagonali svi su ostali elementi \(1\), samo su \(i\)-ti i \(j\)-ti element jednaki \(0\), a dva elementa u \(i\)-tom retku i \(j\)-tom stupcu te u \(j\)-tom retku i \(i\)-tom stupcu jednaka su \(1\).

Za matricu zamjene zahtijeva se da \(i\) i \(j\) nisu jednaki.

Matrica dodavanja

Matrica dodavanja (addition matrix) dobiva se iz jedinične matrice \(I\) tako da se element u \(i\)-tom retku i \(j\)-tom stupcu postavi na \(k\).

\[ T_{ij}(k)=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & & & & & & \\ & \ddots & & & & & \\ & & 1 & \cdots & k & & \\ & & & \ddots & \vdots & & \\ & & & & 1 & & \\ & & & & & \ddots & \\ & & & & & & 1\\ \end{pmatrix} \]

Za matricu dodavanja zahtijeva se da \(i\) i \(j\) nisu jednaki. Ako je \(k\) jednak \(0\), \(T_{ij}(0)\) degenerira u jediničnu matricu \(I\).

Matrica dodavanja gornja je ili donja trokutasta matrica.

Determinante elementarnih matrica

Tri vrste elementarnih matrica imaju determinante:

\[ |D_i(k)|=k \]
\[ |P_{ij}|=-1 \]
\[ |T_{ij}(k)|=1 \]

Budući da je determinanta umnoška kvadratnih matrica jednaka umnošku determinanti, uz ekvivalenciju elementarnih transformacija i množenja matrica opisanu u nastavku, ovo se svojstvo elementarnih matrica može iskoristiti za računanje determinanti.

Elementarne transformacije

Ne samo na kvadratnim matricama nego i na općoj matrici \(A\) mogu se izvoditi elementarne transformacije redaka i elementarne transformacije stupaca, zajedničkim imenom elementarne transformacije.

Elementarnih transformacija redaka, kao i elementarnih transformacija stupaca, ima 3 vrste: množenje (multiplication), zamjena (switching) i dodavanje (addition). Najprije opisujemo elementarne transformacije redaka:

  • \(i\)-ti redak množi se brojem \(k\) različitim od nule: \(B\mapsto D_i(k)B\).
  • \(i\)-ti i \(j\)-ti redak zamjenjuju mjesta: \(B\mapsto P_{ij}B\).
  • \(j\)-ti redak pomnožen s \(k\) dodaje se \(i\)-tom retku: \(B\mapsto T_{ij}(k)B\).

Zamijenimo li u gornjim operacijama retke stupcima, dobivamo elementarne transformacije stupaca.

Među elementarnim transformacijama zamjena se može ostvariti množenjem i dodavanjem. Očito se dodavanje ne može ostvariti množenjem i zamjenom. Uz znanje o determinantama i ekvivalenciju elementarnih transformacija i množenja matrica iz nastavka može se pokazati i da se množenje ne može ostvariti dodavanjem i zamjenom.

Stoga su, u usporedbi sa zamjenom, množenje i dodavanje bitnije operacije. Zamjena je pomoćna operacija uvedena zato da bi se u metodi eliminacije osigurao uredan redoslijed eliminiranja.

Elementarne transformacije i množenje matrica

Može se primijetiti da se sve tri vrste elementarnih matrica dobivaju tako da se na jediničnoj matrici \(I\) izvede jedna odgovarajuća transformacija. U kasnijem odjeljku o linearnim preslikavanjima istaknut će se da između linearnih preslikavanja i matrica postoji korespondencija slična ovoj ovdje.

Bez obzira na to je li matrica \(A\) kvadratna, elementarna transformacija redaka matrice \(A\) ekvivalentna je množenju matrice \(A\) elementarnom matricom slijeva. Elementarna transformacija stupaca matrice \(A\) ekvivalentna je množenju matrice \(A\) elementarnom matricom zdesna.

Operacija množenja

Množenje slijeva matricom množenja \(D_i(k)\) ekvivalentno je množenju \(i\)-tog retka s \(k\). Množenje zdesna matricom množenja \(D_i(k)\) ekvivalentno je množenju \(i\)-tog stupca s \(k\).

Umnožak dijagonalnih matrica opet je dijagonalna matrica; pri množenju dijagonalnih matrica množe se odgovarajući elementi na glavnoj dijagonali. Budući da je jedinična matrica posebna matrica množenja, a matrica množenja zahtijeva da \(k\) nije \(0\), vidimo da se dijagonalna matrica može rastaviti na umnožak matrica množenja čim su svi njezini elementi na glavnoj dijagonali različiti od \(0\).

Za opću dijagonalnu matricu, bez obzira na to jesu li neki elementi \(0\), vrijedi odgovarajući zaključak. Množenje slijeva dijagonalnom matricom ekvivalentno je množenju odgovarajućih redaka nekim faktorom, a faktor je upravo odgovarajući element na glavnoj dijagonali dijagonalne matrice. Množenje zdesna dijagonalnom matricom ista je operacija na odgovarajućim stupcima.

Budući da je determinanta matrice množenja \(D_i(k)\) jednaka \(k\), nakon operacije množenja na retku ili stupcu kvadratne matrice njezina determinanta postaje \(k\) puta veća. Determinanta dijagonalne matrice umnožak je elemenata na glavnoj dijagonali.

Množenje matrica množenja je komutativno, a komutativno je i množenje dijagonalnih matrica; kad se u umnošku pojavljuju samo dijagonalne matrice, redoslijed može biti proizvoljan.

Operacija množenja koja odgovara jediničnoj matrici ostavlja matricu \(A\) nepromijenjenom; u praksi se takva operacija ne izvodi.

Operacija zamjene

Množenje slijeva matricom zamjene \(P_{ij}\) ekvivalentno je zamjeni \(i\)-tog i \(j\)-tog retka. Množenje zdesna matricom zamjene \(P_{ij}\) ekvivalentno je zamjeni \(i\)-tog i \(j\)-tog stupca.

Slično odnosu između matrica množenja i dijagonalnih matrica, ovdje uvodimo pojam permutacijske matrice. Permutacijska matrica kvadratna je matrica koja u svakom retku i svakom stupcu ima točno jednu \(1\), a na ostalim mjestima \(0\). Jedinična matrica \(I\) također je posebna permutacijska matrica.

Permutacijska matrica odgovara permutiranju redaka jedinične matrice \(I\), a jednako tako i permutiranju stupaca jedinične matrice \(I\). Sama jedinična matrica \(I\) odgovara identičkom preslikavanju.

Množenje slijeva permutacijskom matricom ekvivalentno je permutiranju redaka izvorne matrice, a množenje zdesna permutacijskom matricom ekvivalentno je permutiranju stupaca izvorne matrice; pripadna permutacija jednaka je onoj kojom su permutirani retci odnosno stupci jedinične matrice \(I\).

Permutacijske matrice u potpunoj su korespondenciji s permutacijama; multiplikativna grupa permutacijskih matrica izomorfna je grupi permutacija. Prema poznatom teoremu, ako se identičko preslikavanje smatra umnoškom nula transpozicija, svaka se permutacija može rastaviti na umnožak transpozicija, pa se stoga i svaka permutacijska matrica može rastaviti na umnožak matrica zamjene.

Budući da je determinanta matrice zamjene \(-1\), nakon operacije zamjene na retcima ili stupcima kvadratne matrice njezina se determinanta množi s \(-1\).

Množenje matrica zamjene nije komutativno, a ni množenje permutacijskih matrica nije komutativno.

Determinanta permutacijske matrice jednaka je \({(-1)}^p\), gdje je \(p\) broj inverzija permutacije koja odgovara permutacijskoj matrici, tj. broj transpozicija u rastavu permutacije na umnožak transpozicija.

Operacija dodavanja

Množenje slijeva matricom dodavanja \(T_{ij}(k)\) ekvivalentno je dodavanju \(k\)-terostrukog \(j\)-tog retka \(i\)-tom retku. Množenje zdesna matricom dodavanja \(T_{ij}(k)\) ekvivalentno je dodavanju \(k\)-terostrukog \(i\)-tog stupca \(j\)-tom stupcu.

Ako je to teško zapamtiti, može se pogledati koja je operacija na jediničnoj matrici \(I\) dala matricu dodavanja \(T_{ij}(k)\); te dvije stvari odgovaraju jedna drugoj: množenje slijeva operacija je na retcima, množenje zdesna operacija je na stupcima, u skladu s mnemonikom „lijevo redak, desno stupac”.

Budući da je determinanta matrice dodavanja \(1\), nakon operacije dodavanja na kvadratnoj matrici njezina se determinanta ne mijenja.

Množenje matrica dodavanja nije komutativno.

Operacija dodavanja koja odgovara jediničnoj matrici ostavlja matricu \(A\) nepromijenjenom; u praksi se takva operacija ne izvodi.

Gornja trokutasta matrica

Matrica dodavanja gornja je ili donja trokutasta matrica. Budući da su te dvije vrste matrica simetrične s obzirom na glavnu dijagonalu, ovdje razmatramo gornje trokutaste matrice. Zapravo su u ovom primjeru potrebne samo elementarne transformacije redaka, a ne i transformacije stupaca.

Ako su svi elementi na glavnoj dijagonali gornje trokutaste matrice jednaki \(1\), ona se može rastaviti na umnožak niza matrica dodavanja. Redoslijed rastavljanja je sljedeći: najprije se operacija dodavanja izvodi na prvom retku jedinične matrice \(I\), zatim na drugom retku jedinične matrice \(I\), i tako dalje, dok se ne obradi svaki redak.

Budući da množenje matrica dodavanja nije komutativno, redoslijed gornjih operacija ne smije se mijenjati.

Ako su svi elementi na glavnoj dijagonali gornje trokutaste matrice različiti od \(0\), ona se može rastaviti na umnožak niza matrica dodavanja i matrica množenja. Pri obradi svakog retka jedinične matrice \(I\) najprije se na tom retku izvede operacija množenja, čime element na glavnoj dijagonali postaje zadana vrijednost različita od nule.

Ako na glavnoj dijagonali gornje trokutaste matrice postoji \(0\), ona se ne može rastaviti na umnožak niza elementarnih matrica.

Bez obzira na to ima li na glavnoj dijagonali gornje trokutaste matrice \(0\), determinanta gornje trokutaste matrice jednaka je umnošku elemenata na glavnoj dijagonali, jednako kao kod dijagonalnih matrica.

Pretvaranje kvadratne matrice u dijagonalnu operacijama dodavanja

Samo operacijama dodavanja svaka se kvadratna matrica može pretvoriti u dijagonalnu; u ovom su primjeru potrebne i elementarne transformacije redaka i elementarne transformacije stupaca.

Ako u prvom retku i prvom stupcu kvadratne matrice postoji element različit od nule, operacijom dodavanja možemo element u gornjem lijevom kutu učiniti različitim od nule, a zatim elementarnim transformacijama redaka i stupaca sve elemente prvog retka i prvog stupca, osim onog u gornjem lijevom kutu, pretvoriti u \(0\).

Ako su prvi redak i prvi stupac kvadratne matrice već u cijelosti \(0\), izravno prelazimo na drugi redak i drugi stupac.

Ovim se postupkom čak može postići da se svi elementi dijagonalne matrice različiti od nule nalaze u gornjem lijevom kutu.

Ako su prvi redak i prvi stupac kvadratne matrice već u cijelosti \(0\), provjeravamo ima li u preostalim retcima i stupcima elemenata različitih od nule; čim ih ima, operacijom dodavanja neki element prvog retka i prvog stupca možemo učiniti različitim od \(0\) i tako se svesti na početnu situaciju u kojoj je element u gornjem lijevom kutu različit od \(0\).

Jedino kad ni u preostalim retcima i stupcima nema elemenata različitih od nule, element u gornjem lijevom kutu ne može se učiniti različitim od nule; tada je preostala kvadratna matrica već nul-matrica.

Matrica u normalnom obliku

Elementarnim transformacijama svaka se matrica, bez obzira na oblik, može svesti na matricu u normalnom obliku.

Matrica u normalnom obliku ima jediničnu matricu \(I\) kao podmatricu u gornjem lijevom kutu, a svi ostali elementi jednaki su \(0\). Postupak svođenja sličan je pretvaranju kvadratne matrice u dijagonalnu, a dodatno se operacijama množenja elementi različiti od nule u gornjem lijevom kutu pretvaraju u \(1\).

Nakon svođenja matrice na normalni oblik broj elemenata jednakih \(1\) upravo je rang matrice.

Invertibilne matrice

Neka je \(A\) matrica \(n\)-tog reda. Ako postoji matrica \(B\) \(n\)-tog reda takva da je \(AB=BA=I\), matrica \(A\) naziva se invertibilnom (regularnom) ili nesingularnom matricom, a \(B\) se naziva inverznom matricom matrice \(A\) i označava s \(A^{-1}\).

Ako je matrica \(A\) invertibilna, inverzna matrica od \(A\) jednoznačno je određena matricom \(A\).

Inverz \(A^{-1}\) invertibilne matrice \(A\) također je invertibilan, a inverz od \(A^{-1}\) upravo je \(A\).

Umnožak \(AB\) dviju invertibilnih matrica \(A\) i \(B\) također je invertibilan, a njegov je inverz \(B^{-1}A^{-1}\).

Transponirana matrica \(A^T\) invertibilne matrice \(A\) također je invertibilna, a inverz transponirane matrice jednak je transponiranoj matrici inverza.

Inverzi elementarnih matrica

Sve su elementarne matrice invertibilne, a inverz je elementarna matrica iste vrste:

\[ {D_i(k)}^{-1}=D_i\left(\frac{1}{k}\right) \]
\[ P_{ij}^{-1}=P_{ij} \]
\[ T_{ij}(k)^{-1}=T_{ij}(-k) \]

Očito je jedinična matrica \(I\) invertibilna, a njezin je inverz opet \(I\).

Elementarne transformacije čuvaju invertibilnost matrice: matrice prije i poslije transformacije ili su obje invertibilne ili su obje neinvertibilne.

Matrica \(A\) invertibilna je ako i samo ako se može zapisati kao umnožak elementarnih matrica, tj. ako se \(A\) elementarnim transformacijama može pretvoriti u jediničnu matricu \(I\).

Nakon uvođenja determinanti znat ćemo:

Matrica \(A\) invertibilna je ako i samo ako je rang matrice \(A\) jednak \(n\), ako i samo ako je determinanta matrice \(A\) različita od \(0\).

Jednostavan zapis: označimo s \(E_{ij}\) matricu tipa \(n\times n\) kojoj je element u \(i\)-tom retku i \(j\)-tom stupcu jednak \(1\), a ostali su nula; tada je

  • \(D_i(k)=I_n+(k-1)E_{ii}\)
  • \(P_{ij}=I_n-E_{ii}-E_{jj}+E_{ij}+E_{ji}\)
  • \(T_{ij}(k)=I_n+kE_{ij}\)

Taj se zapis može primijeniti i na njihove inverzne matrice.

Primjene

Rješavanje sustava linearnih jednadžbi

Za sustav linearnih jednadžbi koeficijenti uz nepoznanice čine matricu sustava (matricu koeficijenata); dopišemo li s desne strane matrice sustava slobodne članove sustava, dobivamo proširenu matricu sustava.

Primjenom elementarnih transformacija redaka proširena matrica sustava linearnih jednadžbi može se najprije svesti na retčani ešelonski oblik (row echelon form), zatim na reducirani retčani ešelonski oblik (reduced row echelon form), čime je sustav riješen. Ta se metoda naziva rješavanjem sustava linearnih jednadžbi eliminacijom, a Gauss–Jordanova eliminacija opisana kasnije algoritam je eliminacije koji se provodi u određenom redoslijedu.

Računanje determinante

Budući da je determinanta umnoška kvadratnih matrica jednaka umnošku njihovih determinanti, determinante elementarnih matrica lako se računaju, a elementarne transformacije ekvivalentne su množenju elementarnim matricama, elementarne se transformacije koriste i pri računanju determinanti.

Budući da je elementarne transformacije koje se provode u određenom redoslijedu lakše programirati, za računanje determinante može se upotrijebiti i Gauss–Jordanova eliminacija opisana kasnije.

Elementary matrices

The following three kinds of square matrices are called elementary matrices.

Multiplication matrix

A multiplication matrix (scaling matrix) is a special diagonal matrix.

\[ D_i(k)=\operatorname{diag}\{1,\cdots,1,k,1,\cdots,1\} \]

It denotes a diagonal matrix whose \(i\)-th entry on the main diagonal is \(k\), where \(k\) is required not to be \(0\), and all other entries are \(1\).

In particular, when \(k\) is \(1\), \(D_i(1)\) is just the identity matrix \(I\).

Switching matrix

A switching matrix (swap matrix) is a special symmetric matrix.

\[ P_{ij}=\begin{pmatrix} I_{i-1} & & & & \\ & 0 & & 1 & \\ & & I_{j-i-1} & & \\ & 1 & & 0 & \\ & & & & I_{n-j}\\ \end{pmatrix} \]

All entries of a switching matrix are \(1\) or \(0\); on the main diagonal all other entries are \(1\), only the \(i\)-th and \(j\)-th entries are \(0\), and the two entries in row \(i\), column \(j\) and in row \(j\), column \(i\) are \(1\).

A switching matrix requires \(i\) and \(j\) to be different.

Addition matrix

An addition matrix is obtained from the identity matrix \(I\) by setting the entry in row \(i\), column \(j\) to \(k\).

\[ T_{ij}(k)=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & & & & & & \\ & \ddots & & & & & \\ & & 1 & \cdots & k & & \\ & & & \ddots & \vdots & & \\ & & & & 1 & & \\ & & & & & \ddots & \\ & & & & & & 1\\ \end{pmatrix} \]

An addition matrix requires \(i\) and \(j\) to be different. If \(k\) is \(0\), then \(T_{ij}(0)\) degenerates to the identity matrix \(I\).

An addition matrix is an upper triangular or a lower triangular matrix.

Determinants of elementary matrices

The three kinds of elementary matrices have the determinants:

\[ |D_i(k)|=k \]
\[ |P_{ij}|=-1 \]
\[ |T_{ij}(k)|=1 \]

Since the determinant of a product of square matrices equals the product of the determinants, together with the equivalence of elementary operations and matrix multiplication described below, this property of elementary matrices can be used to compute determinants.

Elementary operations

Not only on square matrices but on a general matrix \(A\) one can perform elementary row operations and elementary column operations, collectively called elementary operations.

Elementary row operations, like elementary column operations, come in 3 kinds: multiplication (scaling), switching (swapping), and addition (adding a multiple). We first introduce the elementary row operations:

  • multiply row \(i\) by a nonzero number \(k\): \(B\mapsto D_i(k)B\).
  • swap rows \(i\) and \(j\): \(B\mapsto P_{ij}B\).
  • add \(k\) times row \(j\) to row \(i\): \(B\mapsto T_{ij}(k)B\).

Replacing rows by columns in the operations above gives the elementary column operations.

Among the elementary operations, switching can be realized by multiplication and addition. Obviously, addition cannot be realized by multiplication and switching. Using knowledge of determinants and the equivalence of elementary operations and matrix multiplication described below, one can also show that multiplication cannot be realized by addition and switching.

Therefore, compared with switching, multiplication and addition are the more essential operations. Switching is an auxiliary operation introduced in order to keep the elimination orderly in the elimination method.

Elementary operations and matrix multiplication

One can observe that all three kinds of elementary matrices are obtained by performing one corresponding operation on the identity matrix \(I\). In the later section on linear mappings it is pointed out that there is a correspondence between linear mappings and matrices, similar to the relationship here.

Regardless of whether the matrix \(A\) is square, performing an elementary row operation on \(A\) is equivalent to multiplying \(A\) on the left by an elementary matrix. Performing an elementary column operation on \(A\) is equivalent to multiplying \(A\) on the right by an elementary matrix.

Multiplication operation

Multiplying on the left by a multiplication matrix \(D_i(k)\) is equivalent to multiplying row \(i\) by \(k\). Multiplying on the right by a multiplication matrix \(D_i(k)\) is equivalent to multiplying column \(i\) by \(k\).

The product of diagonal matrices is again a diagonal matrix; to multiply diagonal matrices, multiply the corresponding entries on the main diagonal. Since the identity matrix is a special multiplication matrix, and a multiplication matrix requires \(k\) not to be \(0\), we see that a diagonal matrix can be decomposed into a product of multiplication matrices as long as none of its entries on the main diagonal is \(0\).

For a general diagonal matrix, regardless of whether some entries are \(0\), there is a corresponding conclusion. Multiplying on the left by a diagonal matrix is equivalent to multiplying the corresponding rows by some factors, the factors being exactly the corresponding entries on the main diagonal of the diagonal matrix. Multiplying on the right by a diagonal matrix performs the same operation on the corresponding columns.

Since the determinant of the multiplication matrix \(D_i(k)\) is \(k\), after a multiplication operation on a row or column of a square matrix, its determinant becomes \(k\) times the original. The determinant of a diagonal matrix is the product of the entries on the main diagonal.

Multiplication of multiplication matrices is commutative, and multiplication of diagonal matrices is also commutative; when a product contains only diagonal matrices, the order can be arbitrary.

The multiplication operation corresponding to the identity matrix leaves the matrix \(A\) unchanged; in practice such an operation is not performed.

Switching operation

Multiplying on the left by a switching matrix \(P_{ij}\) is equivalent to swapping rows \(i\) and \(j\). Multiplying on the right by a switching matrix \(P_{ij}\) is equivalent to swapping columns \(i\) and \(j\).

Similar to the relationship between multiplication matrices and diagonal matrices, here we introduce the concept of a permutation matrix. A permutation matrix is a square matrix with exactly one \(1\) in every row and every column and \(0\) elsewhere. The identity matrix \(I\) is also a special permutation matrix.

A permutation matrix corresponds to permuting the rows of the identity matrix \(I\), and equally to permuting the columns of the identity matrix \(I\). The identity matrix \(I\) itself corresponds to the identity transformation.

Multiplying on the left by a permutation matrix is equivalent to permuting the rows of the original matrix, and multiplying on the right by a permutation matrix is equivalent to permuting the columns of the original matrix; the corresponding permutation is the same as the one applied to the rows or columns of the identity matrix \(I\).

Permutation matrices correspond exactly to permutations; the multiplicative group of permutation matrices is isomorphic to the permutation group. By a well-known theorem, if the identity transformation is regarded as the product of zero transpositions, every permutation can be decomposed into a product of transpositions, and therefore every permutation matrix can be decomposed into a product of switching matrices.

Since the determinant of a switching matrix is \(-1\), after a switching operation on the rows or columns of a square matrix, its determinant becomes \(-1\) times the original.

Multiplication of switching matrices is not commutative, and multiplication of permutation matrices is not commutative either.

The determinant of a permutation matrix is \({(-1)}^p\), where \(p\) is the inversion number of the permutation corresponding to the permutation matrix, that is, the number of transpositions in the decomposition of the permutation into a product of transpositions.

Addition operation

Multiplying on the left by an addition matrix \(T_{ij}(k)\) is equivalent to adding \(k\) times row \(j\) to row \(i\). Multiplying on the right by an addition matrix \(T_{ij}(k)\) is equivalent to adding \(k\) times column \(i\) to column \(j\).

If this is hard to remember, one can look at which operation on the identity matrix \(I\) produced the addition matrix \(T_{ij}(k)\); the two correspond to each other: left multiplication is an operation on rows, right multiplication is an operation on columns, in accordance with the mnemonic "row on the left, column on the right".

Since the determinant of an addition matrix is \(1\), after an addition operation on a square matrix, its determinant does not change.

Multiplication of addition matrices is not commutative.

The addition operation corresponding to the identity matrix leaves the matrix \(A\) unchanged; in practice such an operation is not performed.

Upper triangular matrix

An addition matrix is an upper triangular or a lower triangular matrix. Since the two kinds of matrices are symmetric with respect to the main diagonal, we discuss upper triangular matrices here. In fact, in this example only elementary row operations are needed, and no column operations.

If all entries on the main diagonal of an upper triangular matrix are \(1\), it can be decomposed into a product of a sequence of addition matrices. The order of the decomposition is as follows: first perform an addition operation on the first row of the identity matrix \(I\), then perform an addition operation on the second row of the identity matrix \(I\), and so on, until every row has been processed.

Since multiplication of addition matrices is not commutative, the order of the operations above cannot be changed.

If no entry on the main diagonal of an upper triangular matrix is \(0\), it can be decomposed into a product of a sequence of addition matrices and multiplication matrices. When processing each row of the identity matrix \(I\), one can first perform a multiplication operation on that row, so that the entry on the main diagonal becomes the specified nonzero value.

If there is a \(0\) on the main diagonal of an upper triangular matrix, it cannot be decomposed into a product of a sequence of elementary matrices.

Regardless of whether there is a \(0\) on the main diagonal of an upper triangular matrix, the determinant of an upper triangular matrix equals the product of the entries on the main diagonal, just as for diagonal matrices.

Transforming a square matrix into a diagonal matrix by addition operations

Using only addition operations, any square matrix can be transformed into a diagonal matrix; this example requires both elementary row operations and elementary column operations.

If there is a nonzero entry in the first row or the first column of the square matrix, we can make the upper left entry nonzero by means of addition operations, and then, using elementary row and column operations, make all entries of the first row and the first column except the upper left one equal to \(0\).

If the first row and the first column of the square matrix are already all \(0\), we simply look at the second row and the second column.

With this method, one can even require that all nonzero entries of the diagonal matrix be in the upper left corner.

If the first row and the first column of the square matrix are already all \(0\), we check whether the remaining rows and columns contain a nonzero entry; as long as there is one, we can make some entry of the first row or the first column different from \(0\) by an addition operation, thereby reducing to the initial situation in which the upper left entry is not \(0\).

Only when the remaining rows and columns contain no nonzero entries either can the upper left entry not be made nonzero; in that case the remaining square matrix is already the zero matrix.

Normal form matrix

Using elementary operations, any matrix, regardless of shape, can be reduced to a normal form matrix.

A normal form matrix has an identity matrix \(I\) as a submatrix in the upper left corner, and all other entries are \(0\). The reduction method is similar to the operation of transforming a square matrix into a diagonal matrix, and additionally multiplication operations are used to turn the nonzero entries in the upper left corner into \(1\).

After a matrix is reduced to a normal form matrix, the number of entries equal to \(1\) is exactly the rank of the matrix.

Invertible matrices

Let \(A\) be a matrix of order \(n\). If there exists a matrix \(B\) of order \(n\) such that \(AB=BA=I\), then \(A\) is called an invertible matrix or a nonsingular matrix, and \(B\) is called the inverse matrix of \(A\), denoted by \(A^{-1}\).

If the matrix \(A\) is invertible, then the inverse of \(A\) is uniquely determined by \(A\).

The inverse \(A^{-1}\) of an invertible matrix \(A\) is also invertible, and the inverse of \(A^{-1}\) is \(A\).

The product \(AB\) of two invertible matrices \(A\) and \(B\) is also invertible, and its inverse is \(B^{-1}A^{-1}\).

The transpose \(A^T\) of an invertible matrix \(A\) is also invertible, and the inverse of the transpose equals the transpose of the inverse.

Inverses of elementary matrices

All elementary matrices are invertible, and the inverse is an elementary matrix of the same kind:

\[ {D_i(k)}^{-1}=D_i\left(\frac{1}{k}\right) \]
\[ P_{ij}^{-1}=P_{ij} \]
\[ T_{ij}(k)^{-1}=T_{ij}(-k) \]

Obviously the identity matrix \(I\) is invertible, and its inverse is again \(I\).

Elementary operations preserve the invertibility of a matrix: the matrices before and after the operation are either both invertible or both non-invertible.

A matrix \(A\) is invertible if and only if \(A\) can be written as a product of elementary matrices, that is, if and only if it can be transformed into the identity matrix \(I\) by elementary operations.

After determinants are introduced, we will know:

A matrix \(A\) is invertible if and only if the rank of \(A\) is \(n\), if and only if the determinant of \(A\) is not \(0\).

A simple notation: let \(E_{ij}\) denote the \(n\times n\) matrix whose entry in row \(i\), column \(j\) is \(1\) and all other entries are zero; then

  • \(D_i(k)=I_n+(k-1)E_{ii}\)
  • \(P_{ij}=I_n-E_{ii}-E_{jj}+E_{ij}+E_{ji}\)
  • \(T_{ij}(k)=I_n+kE_{ij}\)

This notation can also be applied to their inverse matrices.

Applications

Solving systems of linear equations

For a system of linear equations, the coefficients of the unknowns form the coefficient matrix; appending the constant terms of the system to the right of the coefficient matrix gives the augmented matrix.

Applying elementary row operations, the augmented matrix of a system of linear equations can first be transformed into row echelon form, then into reduced row echelon form, thereby solving the system. This method is called solving systems of linear equations by elimination, and the Gauss–Jordan elimination described later is an elimination algorithm performed in a specific order.

Computing determinants

Since the determinant of a product of square matrices equals the product of their determinants, the determinants of elementary matrices are easy to compute, and elementary operations are equivalent to multiplication by elementary matrices, elementary operations are also used in computing determinants.

Since elementary operations performed in a specific order are easier to program, the Gauss–Jordan elimination algorithm described later can also be used to compute determinants.
