Bellovi brojevi \(B_n\) (Bell numbers), nazvani po Ericu Templeu Bellu, niz su cijelih brojeva iz kombinatorike koji počinje ovako (OEIS A000110):

\[ B_0 = 1,B_1 = 1,B_2=2,B_3=5,B_4=15,B_5=52,B_6=203,\dots \]

\(B_n\) je broj particija skupa kardinalnosti \(n\). Particija skupa \(S\) definira se kao familija u parovima disjunktnih nepraznih podskupova od \(S\) čija je unija \(S\). Na primjer, \(B_3 = 5\) jer skup \({a, b, c}\) s 3 elementa ima 5 različitih particija:

\[ \begin{aligned} &\{ \{a\},\{b\},\{c\}\} \\ &\{ \{a\},\{b,c\}\} \\ &\{ \{b\},\{a,c\}\} \\ &\{ \{c\},\{a,b\}\} \\ &\{ \{a,b,c\}\} \\ \end{aligned} \]

\(B_0\) je 1 jer prazan skup ima točno 1 particiju.

Rekurzivna formula

Bellovi brojevi zadovoljavaju rekurzivnu formulu:

\[ B_{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom{n}{k}B_{k} \]

Dokaz:

\(B_{n+1}\) je broj particija skupa s \(n+1\) elementom. Neka je skup koji pripada broju \(B_n\) jednak \(\{b_1,b_2,b_3,\dots,b_n\}\), a skup koji pripada broju \(B_{n+1}\) jednak \(\{b_1,b_2,b_3,\dots,b_n,b_{n+1}\}\); tada možemo smatrati da \(B_{n+1}\) nastaje iz \(B_{n}\) dodavanjem elementa \(b_{n+1}\). Promotrimo element \(b_{n+1}\).

  • Ako je sam u svojem bloku, preostaje \(n\) elemenata, pa je u tom slučaju broj particija \(\dbinom{n}{n}B_{n}\);

  • ako je u istom bloku s još 1 elementom, preostaje \(n-1\) element, pa je u tom slučaju broj particija \(\dbinom{n}{n-1}B_{n-1}\);

  • ako je u istom bloku s još 2 elementa, preostaje \(n-2\) elemenata, pa je u tom slučaju broj particija \(\dbinom{n}{n-2}B_{n-2}\);

  • …

Nastavljajući tako dobivamo gornju formulu.

Svaki je Bellov broj zbroj odgovarajućih Stirlingovih brojeva druge vrste, jer je Stirlingov broj druge vrste broj načina da se skup kardinalnosti \(n\) podijeli na točno \(k\) nepraznih skupova.

\[ B_{n} = \sum_{k=0}^n{n\brace k} \]

Bellov trokut

Sljedećim postupkom konstruiramo trokutastu matricu (oblikom sličnu Pascalovu trokutu):

  • \(a_{0,0} = 1\);
  • za \(n \ge 1\) prvi član \(n\)-tog retka jednak je posljednjem članu prethodnog retka, tj. \(a_{n,0}=a_{n-1,n-1}\);
  • za \(m,n \ge 1\) \(m\)-ti član \(n\)-tog retka jednak je zbroju broja lijevo od njega i broja gore lijevo od njega, tj. \(a_{n,m}=a_{n,m-1}+a_{n-1,m-1}\).

Dio rezultata izgleda ovako:

\[ \begin{aligned} & 1 \\ & 1\quad\qquad 2 \\ & 2\quad\qquad 3\quad\qquad 5 \\ & 5\quad\qquad 7\quad\qquad 10\,\,\,\qquad 15 \\ & 15\,\,\,\qquad 20\,\,\,\qquad 27\,\,\,\qquad 37\,\,\,\qquad 52 \\ & 52\,\,\,\qquad 67\,\,\,\qquad 87\,\,\,\qquad 114\qquad 151\qquad 203\\ & 203\qquad 255\qquad 322\qquad 409\qquad 523\qquad 674\qquad 877 \\ \end{aligned} \]

Prvi član svakog retka Bellov je broj. Pomoću ovog trokuta Bellovi se brojevi mogu rekurzivno izračunati.

Referentna implementacija 
constexpr int MAXN = 2000 + 5;
int bell[MAXN][MAXN];

void f(int n) {
  bell[0][0] = 1;
  for (int i = 1; i <= n; i++) {
    bell[i][0] = bell[i - 1][i - 1];
    for (int j = 1; j <= i; j++)
      bell[i][j] = bell[i - 1][j - 1] + bell[i][j - 1];
  }
}

MAXN = 2000 + 5
bell = [[0 for i in range(MAXN + 1)] for j in range(MAXN + 1)]

    bell[0][0] = 1
    for i in range(1, n + 1):
        bell[i][0] = bell[i - 1][i - 1]
        for j in range(1, i + 1):
            bell[i][j] = bell[i - 1][j - 1] + bell[i][j - 1]

Eksponencijalna funkcija izvodnica

Promotrimo eksponencijalnu funkciju izvodnicu Bellovih brojeva i njezinu derivaciju:

\[ \begin{aligned} \hat B(x) &= \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_n}{n!}x^n \\ &= 1 + \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_{n+1}}{(n + 1)!}x^{n + 1} \\ \hat B'(x) &= \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_{n+1}}{n!}x^{n} \end{aligned} \]

Iz rekurzivne formule za Bellove brojeve dobivamo:

\[ \frac{B_{n+1}}{n!} = \sum_{k = 0}^{n}\frac{1}{(n-k)!}\frac{B_{k}}{k!} \]

To je izraz oblika konvolucije, pa vrijedi:

\[ \hat B'(x) = \mathrm{e}^x \hat B(x) \]

To je diferencijalna jednadžba čije je rješenje:

\[ \hat B(x) = \exp\left(\mathrm{e}^x + C\right) \]

Naposljetku, za \(x = 0\) vrijedi \(\hat B(x) = 1\); uvrštavanjem dobivamo \(C = -1\), pa zatvoreni oblik eksponencijalne funkcije izvodnice Bellovih brojeva glasi:

\[ \hat B(x) = \exp\left(\mathrm{e}^x - 1\right) \]

Nakon što unaprijed izračunamo prvih \(n\) članova od \(\mathrm{e}^x - 1\), jednim eksponencijalom polinoma dobivamo prvih \(n\) Bellovih brojeva; usko je grlo vremenske složenosti eksponencijal polinoma, pa se može postići vremenska složenost \(O(n \log n)\).

The Bell numbers \(B_n\), named after Eric Temple Bell, are an integer sequence in combinatorics that begins (OEIS A000110):

\[ B_0 = 1,B_1 = 1,B_2=2,B_3=5,B_4=15,B_5=52,B_6=203,\dots \]

\(B_n\) is the number of partitions of a set of cardinality \(n\). A partition of a set \(S\) is defined as a family of pairwise disjoint nonempty subsets of \(S\) whose union is \(S\). For example, \(B_3 = 5\) because the 3-element set \({a, b, c}\) has 5 different partitions:

\[ \begin{aligned} &\{ \{a\},\{b\},\{c\}\} \\ &\{ \{a\},\{b,c\}\} \\ &\{ \{b\},\{a,c\}\} \\ &\{ \{c\},\{a,b\}\} \\ &\{ \{a,b,c\}\} \\ \end{aligned} \]

\(B_0\) is 1 because the empty set has exactly 1 partition.

Recurrence formula

The Bell numbers satisfy the recurrence formula:

\[ B_{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom{n}{k}B_{k} \]

Proof:

\(B_{n+1}\) is the number of partitions of a set with \(n+1\) elements. Let the set for \(B_n\) be \(\{b_1,b_2,b_3,\dots,b_n\}\) and the set for \(B_{n+1}\) be \(\{b_1,b_2,b_3,\dots,b_n,b_{n+1}\}\); then we can think of \(B_{n+1}\) as arising from \(B_{n}\) by adding an element \(b_{n+1}\). Consider the element \(b_{n+1}\).

  • If it is alone in its block, \(n\) elements remain, and the number of partitions in this case is \(\dbinom{n}{n}B_{n}\);

  • if it is in a block with 1 other element, \(n-1\) elements remain, and the number of partitions in this case is \(\dbinom{n}{n-1}B_{n-1}\);

  • if it is in a block with 2 other elements, \(n-2\) elements remain, and the number of partitions in this case is \(\dbinom{n}{n-2}B_{n-2}\);

  • …

Continuing in this way gives the formula above.

Every Bell number is the sum of the corresponding Stirling numbers of the second kind, because the Stirling number of the second kind is the number of ways to partition a set of cardinality \(n\) into exactly \(k\) nonempty sets.

\[ B_{n} = \sum_{k=0}^n{n\brace k} \]

Bell triangle

Construct a triangular matrix (similar in form to Pascal's triangle) as follows:

  • \(a_{0,0} = 1\);
  • for \(n \ge 1\), the first entry of row \(n\) equals the last entry of the previous row, i.e. \(a_{n,0}=a_{n-1,n-1}\);
  • for \(m,n \ge 1\), the \(m\)-th entry of row \(n\) equals the sum of the number to its left and the number to its upper left, i.e. \(a_{n,m}=a_{n,m-1}+a_{n-1,m-1}\).

Part of the result is as follows:

\[ \begin{aligned} & 1 \\ & 1\quad\qquad 2 \\ & 2\quad\qquad 3\quad\qquad 5 \\ & 5\quad\qquad 7\quad\qquad 10\,\,\,\qquad 15 \\ & 15\,\,\,\qquad 20\,\,\,\qquad 27\,\,\,\qquad 37\,\,\,\qquad 52 \\ & 52\,\,\,\qquad 67\,\,\,\qquad 87\,\,\,\qquad 114\qquad 151\qquad 203\\ & 203\qquad 255\qquad 322\qquad 409\qquad 523\qquad 674\qquad 877 \\ \end{aligned} \]

The first entry of each row is a Bell number. This triangle can be used to compute the Bell numbers recursively.

Exponential generating function

Consider the exponential generating function of the Bell numbers and its derivative:

\[ \begin{aligned} \hat B(x) &= \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_n}{n!}x^n \\ &= 1 + \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_{n+1}}{(n + 1)!}x^{n + 1} \\ \hat B'(x) &= \sum_{n = 0}^{+\infty}\frac{B_{n+1}}{n!}x^{n} \end{aligned} \]

From the recurrence formula of the Bell numbers we get:

\[ \frac{B_{n+1}}{n!} = \sum_{k = 0}^{n}\frac{1}{(n-k)!}\frac{B_{k}}{k!} \]

This is a convolution, so:

\[ \hat B'(x) = \mathrm{e}^x \hat B(x) \]

This is a differential equation, whose solution is:

\[ \hat B(x) = \exp\left(\mathrm{e}^x + C\right) \]

Finally, when \(x = 0\) we have \(\hat B(x) = 1\); substituting gives \(C = -1\), and we obtain the closed form of the exponential generating function of the Bell numbers:

\[ \hat B(x) = \exp\left(\mathrm{e}^x - 1\right) \]

After precomputing the first \(n\) terms of \(\mathrm{e}^x - 1\), one polynomial exp yields the first \(n\) Bell numbers; the time complexity bottleneck is the polynomial exp, so a time complexity of \(O(n \log n)\) can be achieved.
