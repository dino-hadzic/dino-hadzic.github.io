Uvod

Homogeni linearno rekurzivni nizovi s konstantnim koeficijentima (poznati i kao C-konačni ili C-rekurzivni nizovi) česta su i osnovna klasa rekurzivnih nizova.

Za niz \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) i njegovu rekurziju

\[ a_n=\sum_{j=1}^{d}c_ja_{n-j},\qquad (n\geq d) \]

u kojoj \(c_j\) nisu svi nula, cilj nam je, uz zadane početne vrijednosti \(a_0,\dots ,a_{d-1}\) i koeficijente rekurzije \(c_1,\dots ,c_d\), izračunati \(a_k\). Ako je \(k\gg d\), želimo brži algoritam.

Ovdje se \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) naziva homogenim linearno rekurzivnim nizom s konstantnim koeficijentima reda \(d\).

Fiduccijin algoritam

Fiduccijin algoritam računa \(a_k\) pomoću ostatka pri dijeljenju polinoma i brzog potenciranja, u vremenu \(O(\mathsf{M}(d)\log k)\), pri čemu \(O(\mathsf{M}(d))\) označava vrijeme množenja dvaju polinoma stupnja \(O(d)\).

Algoritam: konstruiramo polinome \(\Gamma(x):=x^d-\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}c_{d-j}x^j\) i \(A(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}a_jx^j\); tada je

\[ a_k=\left\langle x^k\bmod{\Gamma(x)},A(x)\right\rangle \]

gdje je skalarni produkt definiran kao \(\left\langle \left(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f_jx^j\right),\left(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}g_jx^j\right) \right\rangle :=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f_jg_j\).

Dokaz: definiramo pratilačku matricu (companion matrix) polinoma \(\Gamma(x)\) kao

\[ C_\Gamma:= \begin{bmatrix} &&&c_d \\ 1&&&c_{d-1} \\ &\ddots &&\vdots \\ &&1&c_1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Definiramo polinom \(b(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}b_jx^j\) i

\[ B_b:=\begin{bmatrix}b_0&b_1&\cdots &b_{d-1}\end{bmatrix}^{\intercal} \]

Uočimo da je

\[ \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} &&&c_d \\ 1&&&c_{d-1} \\ &\ddots &&\vdots \\ &&1&c_1 \end{bmatrix}}_{C_\Gamma} \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} b_0 \\ b_1 \\ \vdots \\ b_{d-1} \end{bmatrix}}_{B_b}= \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} c_db_{d-1} \\ b_0+c_{d-1}b_{d-1} \\ \vdots \\ b_{d-2}+c_1b_{d-1} \end{bmatrix}} _ {B_{xb\bmod{\Gamma}}} \]

te

\[ \begin{aligned} C_\Gamma&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^2\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^d\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix}, \\ \left(C_\Gamma\right)^2&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x^2\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^3\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^{d+1}\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix}, \\ \cdots \\ \left(C_\Gamma\right)^k&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x^k\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^{k+1}\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^{k+d}\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

Zapišemo li rekurziju matrično, imamo

\[ \begin{bmatrix} a_{k} \\ a_{k+1} \\ \vdots \\ a_{k+d-1} \end{bmatrix}=\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} &1&& \\ &&\ddots & \\ &&&1 \\ c_d&c_{d-1}&\cdots &c_1 \end{bmatrix}^k} _ {\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{\intercal}\right)^k=\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{k}\right)^{\intercal}} \begin{bmatrix} a_0 \\ a_{1} \\ \vdots \\ a_{d-1} \end{bmatrix} \]

Vidimo da je prvi redak matrice \(\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{k}\right)^{\intercal}\) jednak \(B_{x^k\bmod{\Gamma}}\), pa tvrdnja slijedi iz definicije množenja matrica.

Prikaz racionalnom funkcijom

Za gornji niz \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) sigurno postoji racionalna funkcija

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j \]

pri čemu je \(Q(x)=x^d\Gamma\left(x^{-1}\right)\) i \(\deg{P}<d\). Nazivamo je „racionalnom funkcijom” zato što su \(P(x),Q(x)\) „polinomi”.

Dokaz: za \(P(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}p_jx^j\) i \(Q(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d}q_jx^j\) promotrimo definiciju koeficijenata od \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}\tilde{q}_jx^j\); to je gotovo upravo definicija „dijeljenja” formalnih redova potencija,

\[ \tilde{q}_N= \begin{cases} p_0q_0^{-1},&\text{ if }N=0, \\ \left(p_N-\sum_{j=1}^{N}q_j\tilde{q}_{N-j}\right)\cdot q_0^{-1},&\text{ else if }N<d, \\ -q_0^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{d}q_j\tilde{q}_{N-j},&\text{ otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Dovoljno je staviti

\[ P(x)=\left(\left(\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j\right)\cdot x^d\Gamma\left(x^{-1}\right)\right)\bmod{x^d} \]

pa po definiciji \(\tilde{q}_N\) nužno vrijedi \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j\).

Bostan–Morijev algoritam

Računanje jednog člana

Cilj nam je i dalje, uz zadane gornje polinome \(P(x),Q(x)\), izračunati \(\left\lbrack x^k\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\).

Bostan–Morijev algoritam temelji se na Graeffeovoj iteraciji: za gornje polinome \(P(x),Q(x)\) vrijedi

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\frac{P(x)Q(-x)}{Q(x)Q(-x)}=\frac{U_0(x^2)+xU_1(x^2)}{V(x^2)} \]

Budući da je nazivnik \(V(x^2)\) parna funkcija, u potproblemu treba razmotriti samo jednu stranu

\[ \left\lbrack x^k\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lfloor k/2\right\rfloor}\right\rbrack \frac{U_{k\bmod{2}}(x)}{V(x)} \]

Po cijeni dvaju množenja polinoma problem smanjujemo barem na polovinu izvornoga, a za \(k=0\) očito je \(\left\lbrack x^0\right\rbrack \dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\dfrac{P(0)}{Q(0)}\); vremenska složenost ista je kao gore.

Računanje više uzastopnih članova

Cilj je, uz zadane gornje polinome \(P(x),Q(x)\), izračunati \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\). U računu koji slijedi razmatramo samo koeficijente koji „utječu” na odgovor; to je ključ Bostan–Morijeva algoritma.

Bez smanjenja općenitosti pretpostavimo \(\deg{P}<\deg{Q}\); u suprotnom jednim dijeljenjem s ostatkom problem svodimo na taj slučaj.

Najprije razmotrimo jednostavniji problem:

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\cdot Q(-x) \]

Trebamo izračunati \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{Q},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\), zatim obaviti jedno množenje i izdvojiti koeficijente uz \(x^L,\dots ,x^{R-1}\). Neka je \(V(x^2)=Q(x)Q(-x)\); tada je dovoljno izračunati

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \left\lceil\frac{L-\deg{Q}}{2}\right\rceil,\left\lceil\frac{R}{2}\right\rceil\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{V(x)} \]

da bismo rekonstruirali \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{Q},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\). Nadalje, dovoljno je izračunati \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)}\) pa jednim množenjem s \(P(x)\) dobiti \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\).

Iako gornji algoritam već radi, vremenska složenost svakog rekurzivnog poziva ovisi o \(R-L\); želimo se barem u rekurzivnom računu riješiti \(R-L\). Konkretnije, razmotrimo najprije računanje \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)}\); promotrimo

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \frac{1}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\cdot Q(-x) \]

Trebamo izračunati

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg Q,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)} \]

Za \(V(x^2)=Q(x)Q(-x)\) dovoljno je izračunati

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \lceil (L-\deg Q)/2 \rceil,\lceil (L+\deg Q+1)/2 \rceil\right)}\right\rbrack \frac{1}{V(x)} \]

To je zato što je

\[ \left\lbrack x^{k}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}= \begin{cases} \left\lbrack x^{k/2}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{V(x)},&\text{if }k\equiv 0\pmod{2}, \\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Znamo da \(L+\deg Q\) i \(L-\deg Q\) imaju istu parnost, pa je

\[ \left\lceil \frac{L+\deg Q+1}{2}\right\rceil -\left\lceil \frac{L-\deg Q}{2}\right\rceil = \begin{cases} \deg Q+1,&\text{if }L+\deg Q\equiv 0\pmod{2}, \\ \deg Q,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Tako možemo napisati pseudokod

\[ \begin{array}{ll} &\textbf{Algorithm }\operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}(Q,L)\text{:} \\ &\textbf{Input}\text{: }Q(x)\in\mathbb{C}\left\lbrack x\right\rbrack,L\in\mathbb{Z}\text{.} \\ &\textbf{Output}\text{: }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\text{.} \\ 1&\textbf{if }L\leq 1\textbf{ then return }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1} \\ &\text{Use other algorithm to compute }Q(x)^{-1} \\ 2&V(x^2)\gets Q(x)Q(-x) \\ 3&k\gets \left\lceil \frac{L-\deg Q}{2}\right\rceil \\ 4&(t_k,\dots ,t_{k+\deg Q})\gets \operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}\left(V,k\right) \\ 5&T(x)\gets x^{(L-\deg Q)\bmod{2}}\sum_{j=0}^{\deg Q}t_{j+k}x^{2j} \\ 6&\textbf{return }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \deg Q,2\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack T(x)Q(-x) \end{array} \]

No sam taj algoritam nije dovoljan: trebamo iznova pronaći racionalnu funkciju i izračunati više koeficijenata.

Pronalaženje novog prikaza racionalnom funkcijom

Znamo sam \(Q(x)\) i dio uzastopnih koeficijenata od \(Q(x)^{-1}\), npr. \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\) uz \(L\geq 0\), a želimo izračunati \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L+\deg Q,L+2\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\). To je ekvivalentno traženju nekog \(P(x)\) s \(\deg P< \deg Q\) takvog da se prvih \(\deg Q\) članova od \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\) podudara s \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\). Jednostavno rečeno: rekurzivna relacija (nazivnik racionalne funkcije) ostaje nepromijenjena, mi samo mijenjamo početne vrijednosti (brojnik racionalne funkcije).

Konkretno, promotrimo

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j \]

Želimo rekurziju pomaknuti za \(n\) članova unaprijed, tj.

\[ \sum_{j\geq n}a_jx^{j-n}=\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)x^n}-\frac{Q(x)\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_jx^j}{Q(x)x^n} \]

Najprije jednim pozivom \(\operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}(Q,L-\deg{P})\) izračunamo \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},L-\deg{P}+\deg{Q}+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\), zatim proširivanjem i spajanjem dobijemo \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},L+\deg{Q}\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\), pa iznova izračunamo brojnik takav da je

\[ \frac{\widetilde{P}(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}\left(\left\lbrack x^{L+j}\right\rbrack \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right)x^j \]

Na kraju dijeljenjem formalnih redova potencija izračunamo \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack 0,R-L\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{\widetilde{P}(x)}{Q(x)}\); vrijeme je \(O(\mathsf{M}(d)\log L+\mathsf{M}(R-L))\).

Introduction

Homogeneous linearly recurrent sequences with constant coefficients (also called C-finite or C-recursive sequences) are a common and basic class of recurrent sequences.

For a sequence \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) and its recurrence

\[ a_n=\sum_{j=1}^{d}c_ja_{n-j},\qquad (n\geq d) \]

where the \(c_j\) are not all zero, our goal is to compute \(a_k\) given the initial values \(a_0,\dots ,a_{d-1}\) and the coefficients \(c_1,\dots ,c_d\) of the recurrence. If \(k\gg d\), we want a faster algorithm.

Here \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) is called a homogeneous linearly recurrent sequence with constant coefficients of order \(d\).

Fiduccia's algorithm

Fiduccia's algorithm computes \(a_k\) using polynomial modulo and binary exponentiation in \(O(\mathsf{M}(d)\log k)\) time, where \(O(\mathsf{M}(d))\) denotes the time to multiply two polynomials of degree \(O(d)\).

Algorithm: construct the polynomials \(\Gamma(x):=x^d-\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}c_{d-j}x^j\) and \(A(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}a_jx^j\); then

\[ a_k=\left\langle x^k\bmod{\Gamma(x)},A(x)\right\rangle \]

where the inner product is defined as \(\left\langle \left(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f_jx^j\right),\left(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}g_jx^j\right) \right\rangle :=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f_jg_j\).

Proof: we define the companion matrix of \(\Gamma(x)\) as

\[ C_\Gamma:= \begin{bmatrix} &&&c_d \\ 1&&&c_{d-1} \\ &\ddots &&\vdots \\ &&1&c_1 \end{bmatrix} \]

We define the polynomial \(b(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}b_jx^j\) and

\[ B_b:=\begin{bmatrix}b_0&b_1&\cdots &b_{d-1}\end{bmatrix}^{\intercal} \]

Observe that

\[ \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} &&&c_d \\ 1&&&c_{d-1} \\ &\ddots &&\vdots \\ &&1&c_1 \end{bmatrix}}_{C_\Gamma} \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} b_0 \\ b_1 \\ \vdots \\ b_{d-1} \end{bmatrix}}_{B_b}= \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} c_db_{d-1} \\ b_0+c_{d-1}b_{d-1} \\ \vdots \\ b_{d-2}+c_1b_{d-1} \end{bmatrix}} _ {B_{xb\bmod{\Gamma}}} \]

and

\[ \begin{aligned} C_\Gamma&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^2\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^d\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix}, \\ \left(C_\Gamma\right)^2&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x^2\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^3\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^{d+1}\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix}, \\ \cdots \\ \left(C_\Gamma\right)^k&=\begin{bmatrix}B_{x^k\bmod{\Gamma}}&B_{x^{k+1}\bmod{\Gamma}}&\cdots &B_{x^{k+d}\bmod{\Gamma}}\end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

Writing the recurrence in matrix form we have

\[ \begin{bmatrix} a_{k} \\ a_{k+1} \\ \vdots \\ a_{k+d-1} \end{bmatrix}=\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} &1&& \\ &&\ddots & \\ &&&1 \\ c_d&c_{d-1}&\cdots &c_1 \end{bmatrix}^k} _ {\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{\intercal}\right)^k=\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{k}\right)^{\intercal}} \begin{bmatrix} a_0 \\ a_{1} \\ \vdots \\ a_{d-1} \end{bmatrix} \]

We see that the first row of \(\left(\left(C_\Gamma\right)^{k}\right)^{\intercal}\) is \(B_{x^k\bmod{\Gamma}}\), and the claim follows from the definition of matrix multiplication.

Representation as a rational function

For the sequence \(\left(a_j\right)_{j\geq 0}\) above there always exists a rational function

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j \]

with \(Q(x)=x^d\Gamma\left(x^{-1}\right)\) and \(\deg{P}<d\). We call it a “rational function” because \(P(x),Q(x)\) are “polynomials”.

Proof: for \(P(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}p_jx^j\) and \(Q(x):=\sum_{j=0}^{d}q_jx^j\) consider the definition of the coefficients of \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}\tilde{q}_jx^j\); this is almost exactly the definition of “division” of formal power series,

\[ \tilde{q}_N= \begin{cases} p_0q_0^{-1},&\text{ if }N=0, \\ \left(p_N-\sum_{j=1}^{N}q_j\tilde{q}_{N-j}\right)\cdot q_0^{-1},&\text{ else if }N<d, \\ -q_0^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{d}q_j\tilde{q}_{N-j},&\text{ otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

We only need to set

\[ P(x)=\left(\left(\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j\right)\cdot x^d\Gamma\left(x^{-1}\right)\right)\bmod{x^d} \]

and then, by the definition of \(\tilde{q}_N\), necessarily \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j\).

Bostan–Mori algorithm

Computing a single term

Our goal is still, given the polynomials \(P(x),Q(x)\) above, to compute \(\left\lbrack x^k\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\).

The Bostan–Mori algorithm is based on Graeffe's iteration: for the polynomials \(P(x),Q(x)\) above we have

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\frac{P(x)Q(-x)}{Q(x)Q(-x)}=\frac{U_0(x^2)+xU_1(x^2)}{V(x^2)} \]

Since the denominator \(V(x^2)\) is an even function, the subproblem only needs to consider one side

\[ \left\lbrack x^k\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lfloor k/2\right\rfloor}\right\rbrack \frac{U_{k\bmod{2}}(x)}{V(x)} \]

At the cost of two polynomial multiplications we reduce the problem to at least half of the original, and for \(k=0\) clearly \(\left\lbrack x^0\right\rbrack \dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\dfrac{P(0)}{Q(0)}\); the time complexity is the same as above.

Computing several consecutive terms

The goal is, given the polynomials \(P(x),Q(x)\) above, to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\). In the computation below we only consider the coefficients that “affect” the answer; this is the key of the Bostan–Mori algorithm.

We may assume \(\deg{P}<\deg{Q}\); otherwise one division with remainder brings the problem back to this case.

We first consider the simpler problem:

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\cdot Q(-x) \]

We need to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{Q},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\), then do one multiplication and extract the coefficients of \(x^L,\dots ,x^{R-1}\). Let \(V(x^2)=Q(x)Q(-x)\); then we only need to compute

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \left\lceil\frac{L-\deg{Q}}{2}\right\rceil,\left\lceil\frac{R}{2}\right\rceil\right)}\right\rbrack\frac{1}{V(x)} \]

to recover \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{Q},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\). Furthermore, we only need to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)}\) and then do one multiplication with \(P(x)\) to obtain \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,R\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\).

Although the algorithm above already works, the time complexity of each recursive call depends on \(R-L\); we would like to get rid of \(R-L\) at least in the recursive computation. More specifically, we first consider computing \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)}\); consider

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \frac{1}{Q(x)}=\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}\cdot Q(-x) \]

We need to compute

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg Q,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack \dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)} \]

Then for \(V(x^2)=Q(x)Q(-x)\) we only need to compute

\[ \left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \lceil (L-\deg Q)/2 \rceil,\lceil (L+\deg Q+1)/2 \rceil\right)}\right\rbrack \frac{1}{V(x)} \]

This is because

\[ \left\lbrack x^{k}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{Q(x)Q(-x)}= \begin{cases} \left\lbrack x^{k/2}\right\rbrack\dfrac{1}{V(x)},&\text{if }k\equiv 0\pmod{2}, \\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

We know that \(L+\deg Q\) and \(L-\deg Q\) have the same parity, so

\[ \left\lceil \frac{L+\deg Q+1}{2}\right\rceil -\left\lceil \frac{L-\deg Q}{2}\right\rceil = \begin{cases} \deg Q+1,&\text{if }L+\deg Q\equiv 0\pmod{2}, \\ \deg Q,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Thus we can write the pseudocode

\[ \begin{array}{ll} &\textbf{Algorithm }\operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}(Q,L)\text{:} \\ &\textbf{Input}\text{: }Q(x)\in\mathbb{C}\left\lbrack x\right\rbrack,L\in\mathbb{Z}\text{.} \\ &\textbf{Output}\text{: }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\text{.} \\ 1&\textbf{if }L\leq 1\textbf{ then return }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1} \\ &\text{Use other algorithm to compute }Q(x)^{-1} \\ 2&V(x^2)\gets Q(x)Q(-x) \\ 3&k\gets \left\lceil \frac{L-\deg Q}{2}\right\rceil \\ 4&(t_k,\dots ,t_{k+\deg Q})\gets \operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}\left(V,k\right) \\ 5&T(x)\gets x^{(L-\deg Q)\bmod{2}}\sum_{j=0}^{\deg Q}t_{j+k}x^{2j} \\ 6&\textbf{return }\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack \deg Q,2\deg Q+1\right)}\right\rbrack T(x)Q(-x) \end{array} \]

But this algorithm alone is not enough: we need to find a new rational function and compute more coefficients.

Finding a new rational function representation

We know \(Q(x)\) itself and a slice of consecutive coefficients of \(Q(x)^{-1}\), say \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\) with \(L\geq 0\), and we want to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L+\deg Q,L+2\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\). This is equivalent to finding some \(P(x)\) with \(\deg P< \deg Q\) such that the first \(\deg Q\) terms of \(\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\) coincide with \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L,L+\deg Q\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\). Simply put: the recurrence relation (the denominator of the rational function) stays the same; all we do is change the initial values (the numerator of the rational function).

Specifically, consider

\[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_jx^j \]

We now want to advance the recurrence by \(n\) terms, that is

\[ \sum_{j\geq n}a_jx^{j-n}=\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)x^n}-\frac{Q(x)\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_jx^j}{Q(x)x^n} \]

We first use one call of \(\operatorname{Slice-Coefficients}(Q,L-\deg{P})\) to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},L-\deg{P}+\deg{Q}+1\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\), then we extend and merge to get \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack L-\deg{P},L+\deg{Q}\right)}\right\rbrack Q(x)^{-1}\), and then recompute a numerator such that

\[ \frac{\widetilde{P}(x)}{Q(x)}=\sum_{j\geq 0}\left(\left\lbrack x^{L+j}\right\rbrack \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right)x^j \]

Finally we use division of formal power series to compute \(\left\lbrack x^{\left\lbrack 0,R-L\right)}\right\rbrack\dfrac{\widetilde{P}(x)}{Q(x)}\); the time is \(O(\mathsf{M}(d)\log L+\mathsf{M}(R-L))\).
