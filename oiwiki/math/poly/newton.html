Opis

Zadan je polinom \(G\left(x, y\right)\) i poznato je da polinom \(f\left(x\right)\) zadovoljava:

\[ G\left(x, f\left(x\right)\right)\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

te da postoji vrijednost \(f_1\) takva da \(G\left(x, y\right)\) zadovoljava sljedeće uvjete:

  • \(G(0, f_1) = 0\);
  • \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial y}(0, f_1) \neq 0\).

Treba odrediti \(f\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{n}\).

Newtonova metoda

Razmotrimo udvostručavanje.

Najprije, za \(n=1\) rješenje jednadžbe \(\left[x^{0}\right]G\left(x, f\left(x\right)\right)=0\) treba odrediti zasebno; \(f_1\) iz pretpostavke upravo je jedno takvo rješenje.

Pretpostavimo da smo već dobili rješenje \(f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\) i da tražimo rješenje \(f\left(x\right) = f_n\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{n}\).

Razvijemo li \(G\left(x, f(x)\right)\) u Taylorov red po \(f(x)\) oko \(f(x)=f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\), dobivamo:

\[ \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{\frac{\partial^i G}{\partial y^i}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{i!}\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Budući da je stupanj najnižeg nenul člana polinoma \(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\) barem \(\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil\), vrijedi:

\[ \forall 2\leqslant i:\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Stoga:

\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{\frac{\partial^i G}{\partial y^i}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{i!}\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}&\equiv G\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)+\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)\left[f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right]\\ &\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \end{aligned} \]
\[ f_n\left(x\right)\equiv f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)-\frac{G\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}\pmod{x^{n}} \]

odnosno

\[ f_{2n}\left(x\right)\equiv f_n\left(x\right)-\frac{G\left(x, f_n\left(x\right)\right)}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_n\left(x\right)\right)}\pmod{x^{2n}} \]

Primjeri zadataka

Inverz polinoma

Neka je zadana funkcija \(h\left(x\right)\); imamo:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=\frac{1}{y}-h\left(x\right) \]

Primjenom Newtonove metode dobivamo:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{1/f_{n}\left(x\right)-h\left(x\right)}{-1/f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv 2f_{n}\left(x\right)-f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)h\left(x\right)&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Vremenska složenost

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Korijen polinoma

Neka je zadana funkcija \(h\left(x\right)\); imamo:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=y^{2}-h\left(x\right)\equiv 0 \]

Primjenom Newtonove metode dobivamo:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)-h\left(x\right)}{2f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv\frac{f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)+h\left(x\right)}{2f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Vremenska složenost

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Eksponencijal polinoma

Neka je zadana funkcija \(h\left(x\right)\); imamo:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=\ln{y}-h\left(x\right) \]

Primjenom Newtonove metode dobivamo:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{\ln{f_{n}\left(x\right)}-h\left(x\right)}{1/f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)\left(1-\ln{f_{n}\left(x\right)+h\left(x\right)}\right)&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Vremenska složenost

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Demonstracija ručnog računanja

Radi lakšeg razumijevanja, ovdje na nekoliko primjera demonstriramo tijek algoritma.

Kvadratni korijen kompleksnog polinoma modulo polinom

Pretpostavimo da je \(h\) kompleksni polinom koji nije djeljiv s \(x\) (ima slobodni član) i da tražimo njegov kvadratni korijen modulo \(x^n\).

Imamo jednadžbu:

\[ G\left(f(x)\right) = f^2(x)-h(x) \equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Taylorovim razvojem \(G\) dobivamo sljedeći izraz. Uočimo da je ovo razvoj po \(f\), pa su sve derivacije parcijalne derivacije po \(f\), a \(x\) se ovdje tretira kao konstanta.

\[ G(f(x)) = \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{G^{\left(i\right)}\left(f_{0}(x)\right)}{i!}\left(f(x)-f_{0}(x)\right)^{i} = G(f_0(x)) + 2f_0(x)(f(x)-f_0(x)) + (f(x)-f_0(x))^2 \]

Računamo udvostručavanjem. Pretpostavimo da su međurezultati udvostručavanja \(f_0(x), f_1(x), \ldots, f_j(x)\), ili, matematički strogo, da je \(f_j(x)\) kompleksni polinom koji zadovoljava \(G(f_j(x))\equiv 0\pmod{x^{2^j}}\) te, radi jedinstvenosti, istodobno zadovoljava sljedeća dva uvjeta:

  • stupanj \(f_{j}(x)\) ne prelazi \(x^{2^j}\);
  • \(f_{j+k}(x)-f_j(x)\equiv 0\pmod{x^{2^j}}\) za sve \(k\).

Uvrštavanjem \(f_{j+1}(x)\) i \(f_j(x)\) u gornji izraz dobivamo:

\[ G(f_{j+1}(x)) = G(f_j(x)) + 2f_j(f_{j+1}(x)-f_j(x)) + (f_{j+1}(x)-f_{j}(x))^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{x^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Očito \(f_{j+1}(x)-f_j(x)\) mora biti višekratnik od \(x^{2^j}\). Tako dobivamo

\[ f_{j+1}(x) \equiv f_j(x) - \frac{f_j^2(x)-h(x)}{2f_j(x)} \equiv \frac{f_j(x)^2 + h(x)}{2f_j(x)} \pmod{x^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Ako \(f_j(x)\) postoji, tada \(2f_j(x)\) nije djeljiv s \(x\) (ima slobodni član), pa nužno ima inverz modulo \(x^{2^{j+1}}\). Stoga niz \(f_0,f_1\ldots,f_j\) postoji ako i samo ako postoji \(f_0\). Kvadratni korijen kompleksnog polinoma \(h(x)\) koji nije djeljiv s \(x\) modulo \(x\) sigurno postoji, jer je \(h(x)\) modulo \(x\) obični kompleksni broj različit od nule, koji sigurno ima dva kvadratna korijena. Zato se ovaj algoritam može primijeniti na sve \(h(x)\) sa slobodnim članom.

Za primjer uzmimo \(h(x)=x+1\); računanje izgleda ovako:

  • \(f_0(x)=1\),\(f_1(x)=\dfrac{1^2+x+1}{2\times 1}\mod x^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}x+1\),\(f_2(x)=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)^2+x+1}{2\times \left(\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)}\mod x^4 = \dfrac{1}{16}x^3-\dfrac{1}{8}x^2+\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\),\(\ldots\)
  • \(f_0(x)=-1\),\(f_1(x)=\dfrac{(-1)^2+x+1}{2\times (-1)}\mod x^2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}x-1\),\(\ldots\) (jednako prethodnom s promijenjenim predznakom)

Može se provjeriti da su oba niza ispravni nizovi polinoma modularnih kvadratnih korijena.

Kvadratni korijen cijelog broja modulo potencija prostog broja

Algoritam Newtonove iteracije može se prenijeti i na slučaj cijelih brojeva modulo potencija prostog broja. Pretpostavimo da je \(h\) „zgodan” cijeli broj koji nije djeljiv s 3. („Zgodan” znači „sigurno ima rješenje”; točan uvjet navodimo kasnije.) Pretpostavimo da želimo izračunati kvadratni korijen \(f\) od \(h\) modulo \(3^n\). Imamo jednadžbu:

\[ G\left(f\right) = f^2-h \equiv 0\pmod{3^{n}} \]

Taylorovim razvojem \(G\) dobivamo:

\[ G(f) = \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{G^{\left(i\right)}\left(f_{0}\right)}{i!}\left(f-f_{0}\right)^{i} = G(f_0) + 2f_0(f-f_0) + (f-f_0)^2 \]

Računamo udvostručavanjem. Pretpostavimo da su međurezultati dobiveni udvostručavanjem \(f_0, f_1, \ldots, f_j\), ili, strogo rečeno, da je \(f_j\) cijeli broj koji zadovoljava \(G(f_j)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2^j}}\) te, radi jedinstvenosti, istodobno zadovoljava sljedeća dva uvjeta:

  • \(0 < f_{j} < 3^{2^j}\);
  • \(f_{j+k}-f_j\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2^j}}\) za sve \(k\).

Uvrštavanjem \(f_{j+1}\) i \(f_j\) u gornji izraz dobivamo:

\[ G(f_{j+1}) = G(f_j) + 2f_j(f_{j+1}-f_j) + (f_{j+1}-f_{j})^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{3^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Očito \(f_{j+1}-f_j\) mora biti višekratnik od \(3^{2^j}\). Tako dobivamo

\[ f_{j+1} \equiv f_j - \frac{f_j^2-h}{2f_j} \equiv \frac{f_j^2 + h}{2f_j} \pmod{3^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Ako \(f_j\) postoji, tada \(2f_j\) nije djeljiv s \(3\), pa nužno ima inverz modulo \(3^{2^{j+1}}\). Stoga niz \(f_0,f_1\ldots,f_j\) postoji ako i samo ako postoji \(f_0\). Kvadratni korijen cijelog broja \(h\) koji nije djeljiv s 3 modulo \(3\) ili ne postoji ili ih ima dva. Dakle, jedini uvjet da cijeli algoritam radi jest da \(h\) ima kvadratni korijen modulo \(3\).

Ovdje za konkretan primjer računanja uzimamo \(h=46\).

  • \(f_0=1\),\(f_1=\dfrac{1^2+46}{2\times 1}\mod 9 = 1\),\(f_2=\dfrac{1^2+46}{2\times 1}\mod 81 = 64\),\(f_3=\dfrac{64^2+46}{2\times 64}\mod 6561 = 955\),\(\ldots\)
  • \(f_0=2\),\(f_1=\dfrac{2^2+46}{2\times 2}\mod 9 = 8\),\(f_2=\dfrac{8^2+46}{2\times 8}\mod 81 = 17\),\(f_3=\dfrac{17^2+46}{2\times 17}\mod 6561 = 5606\),\(\ldots\) (jednako prethodnom s promijenjenim predznakom)

Može se provjeriti da su oba niza ispravni nizovi modularnih kvadratnih korijena.

Algebarski dokaz

Ovaj odjeljak proširuje prethodni tekst: jezikom apstraktne algebre dokazujemo da, čim \(f\) zadovoljava uvjet početnog rješenja, Newtonova metoda daje rješenje za svaki \(n\) i da se njome mogu dobiti sva rješenja.

Dokaz postojanja rješenja

Lema 1

Neka integralna domena \(R\) ima polinom ili formalni red potencija \(f(X) = \sum_{i\geq 0}a_iX^i\) te \(r,p\in R\) takve da je \(f(r)\in Rp\) (tj. \(r\) je korijen od \(f(X)\) modulo \(p\)) i da je \(f'(r)\in R\) invertibilan modulo \(p\). Ovdje je \(f'(X) := \sum_{i\geq 0}(i+1)a_{i+1}X^i\) formalna derivacija od \(f(X)\). Tada je \(f\left(r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\right) \equiv 0\pmod {p^2}\).

Dokaz

Za svaki \(s\in R\),

\[ \begin{aligned} f(r+sp) &= \sum_{i\geq 0}a_i(r+sp)^i \\ &= \sum_{i\geq 0}a_ir^i + sp\sum_{i\geq 1}ia_ir^{i-1} + s^2p^2\left(\ldots\right) \\ &= f(r) + spf'(r) + s^2p^2\left(\frac{f''(r)}{2!} + \cdots\right), \end{aligned} \]

pa je

\[ f(r+sp) \in Rp^2 \iff f(r)+f'(r)sp \in Rp^2 \]

Budući da je \(f(r)\in Rp\) i da je \(f'(r)\) invertibilan, dovoljno je uzeti \(sp = -\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\), gdje je \(\dfrac{1}{f'(r)}\) inverz modulo \(p^2\). Kako je \(f'(r)\) invertibilan modulo \(p\), nužno ima inverz i modulo \(p^2\): neka su \(a,b,c\in R\) takvi da je \(af'(r) = bp+1\) i \(f(r)=cp\); tada je \(\left(a^2f'(r)-2\right)f'(r) = b^2p^2+1\), pa možemo uzeti \(s=c(2-a^2f'(r))\).

Za prsten polinoma \(k[X]\) nad poljem \(k\), neka su \(G(X, Y)\in k[X, Y]\) i \(f_n\in k[X]\) takvi da je \(G(X, f_n(X))\in k[X]X^n\); primjenom leme 1 dobivamo

\[ G\left(X, f_n(X) - \frac{G(X, f_n(X))}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n(X))} \right)\equiv 0 \pmod {X^{2n}} \]

Za početni uvjet udvostručavanja dovoljno je da postoji \(f_1\in k\) takav da je \(G(X, f_1)\equiv 0\pmod X\) i \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_1)\not\equiv 0\pmod X\). Potonji uvjet osigurava da \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}\) ima nenul slobodni član, a budući da \(X\left| \dfrac{G(X, f_n(X))}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n(X))} \right.\), za svaki \(n\) je \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n)\) uvijek invertibilan modulo \(X^n\), čime je ispunjen i uvjet za sljedeću iteraciju.

Dokaz da se dobivaju sva rješenja

Lema 2

Neka je \(R\) UFD, a \(f,r,p\) definirani kao u lemi 1. Tada je \(r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\) iz leme 1 jedinstvena vrijednost \(x\) modulo \(p^{2}\) koja zadovoljava sljedeća dva uvjeta:

  • \(f(x)\in Rp^{2}\)
  • \(x-r\in Rp\)

tj.

\[ \forall x\in R,\qquad p^2\mid f(x)\wedge p\mid (x-r) \implies x\equiv r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)} \pmod {p^2} \]
Dokaz

Stavimo \(s = -\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)p}\) i \(u = r+sp\); lema 1 jamči da \(u\) zadovoljava oba uvjeta i da je \(f(r) + f'(r)sp \in Rp^{2}\). Neka je \(v\) vrijednost koja zadovoljava gornje uvjete; tada je \(v = r+tp\) i \(f(r) + f'(r)tp \in Rp^{2}\). Odatle je \(f'(r)(t-s)p\in Rp^{2}\) i \(v-u\in Rp^{2}\).

Newtonova metoda jamči da se dobivaju sva rješenja modulo \(X^{2^n}\). Pretpostavimo da je \(G(X, h)\equiv 0\pmod {X^{2^n}}\); stavimo \(h_{2^i} := h\pmod {X^{2^i}}\), zatim uzmimo \(f_1 = h_1\) i primijenimo Newtonovu metodu; prema lemi 2 dobivamo \(f_{2^i} \equiv h_{2^i}\pmod {X^{2^i}}\), pa je nužno \(f_{2^n} = h\).

Gornje razmatranje pokazuje i da je, kad je \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial y}(0, y)\) uvijek invertibilan, broj rješenja jednadžbe \(G(X, f)\equiv 0\pmod {X^n}\) jednak broju rješenja jednadžbe \(G(0, f)\equiv 0\pmod X\). Taj zaključak nije trivijalan. Pogledajmo sljedeći primjer.

Primjer u kojem Newtonova metoda ne vrijedi, a broj rješenja raste sa stupnjem

Modulo \(X\) jedini kvadratni korijen od \(X^2\) je \(0\), ali modulo \(X^4\) kvadratni korijeni od \(X^2\) su \(X, -X, X^3+X, \ldots\)

Description

Given a polynomial \(G\left(x, y\right)\), it is known that a polynomial \(f\left(x\right)\) satisfies:

\[ G\left(x, f\left(x\right)\right)\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

and that there exists a value \(f_1\) such that \(G\left(x, y\right)\) satisfies the following conditions:

  • \(G(0, f_1) = 0\);
  • \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial y}(0, f_1) \neq 0\).

Find \(f\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{n}\).

Newton's Method

Consider doubling.

First, for \(n=1\) the solution of \(\left[x^{0}\right]G\left(x, f\left(x\right)\right)=0\) has to be found separately; the \(f_1\) from the assumption is exactly such a solution.

Suppose we have already obtained the solution \(f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\), and we want the solution \(f\left(x\right) = f_n\left(x\right)\) modulo \(x^{n}\).

Expanding \(G\left(x, f(x)\right)\) as a Taylor series in \(f(x)\) around \(f(x)=f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\), we get:

\[ \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{\frac{\partial^i G}{\partial y^i}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{i!}\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Since the degree of the lowest nonzero term of \(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\) is at least \(\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil\), we have:

\[ \forall 2\leqslant i:\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Therefore:

\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{\frac{\partial^i G}{\partial y^i}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{i!}\left(f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)^{i}&\equiv G\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)+\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)\left[f\left(x\right)-f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right]\\ &\equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \end{aligned} \]
\[ f_n\left(x\right)\equiv f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)-\frac{G\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_{\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}\right\rceil}\left(x\right)\right)}\pmod{x^{n}} \]

or

\[ f_{2n}\left(x\right)\equiv f_n\left(x\right)-\frac{G\left(x, f_n\left(x\right)\right)}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}\left(x, f_n\left(x\right)\right)}\pmod{x^{2n}} \]

Examples

Polynomial inverse

Let the given function be \(h\left(x\right)\); we have:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=\frac{1}{y}-h\left(x\right) \]

Applying Newton's Method gives:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{1/f_{n}\left(x\right)-h\left(x\right)}{-1/f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv 2f_{n}\left(x\right)-f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)h\left(x\right)&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Time complexity

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Polynomial square root

Let the given function be \(h\left(x\right)\); we have:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=y^{2}-h\left(x\right)\equiv 0 \]

Applying Newton's Method gives:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)-h\left(x\right)}{2f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv\frac{f_{n}^{2}\left(x\right)+h\left(x\right)}{2f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Time complexity

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Polynomial exponential

Let the given function be \(h\left(x\right)\); we have:

\[ G\left(x, y\right)=\ln{y}-h\left(x\right) \]

Applying Newton's Method gives:

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{2n}\left(x\right)&\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)-\frac{\ln{f_{n}\left(x\right)}-h\left(x\right)}{1/f_{n}\left(x\right)}&\pmod{x^{2n}}\\ &\equiv f_{n}\left(x\right)\left(1-\ln{f_{n}\left(x\right)+h\left(x\right)}\right)&\pmod{x^{2n}} \end{aligned} \]

Time complexity

\[ T\left(n\right)=T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)+O\left(n\log{n}\right)=O\left(n\log{n}\right) \]

Worked examples by hand

To make things easier to understand, here we demonstrate the flow of the algorithm on a few examples.

Square root of a complex polynomial modulo a polynomial

Suppose \(h\) is a complex polynomial not divisible by \(x\) (it has a constant term), and we want its square root modulo \(x^n\).

We have the equation:

\[ G\left(f(x)\right) = f^2(x)-h(x) \equiv 0\pmod{x^{n}} \]

Taylor-expanding \(G\) gives the following expression. Note that this is an expansion in \(f\), so the derivatives are all partial derivatives with respect to \(f\), and \(x\) is treated as a constant here.

\[ G(f(x)) = \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{G^{\left(i\right)}\left(f_{0}(x)\right)}{i!}\left(f(x)-f_{0}(x)\right)^{i} = G(f_0(x)) + 2f_0(x)(f(x)-f_0(x)) + (f(x)-f_0(x))^2 \]

We compute by doubling. Suppose the intermediate results of the doubling are \(f_0(x), f_1(x), \ldots, f_j(x)\), or, mathematically rigorously, \(f_j(x)\) is a complex polynomial satisfying \(G(f_j(x))\equiv 0\pmod{x^{2^j}}\) which, for uniqueness, also satisfies the following two conditions:

  • the degree of \(f_{j}(x)\) does not exceed \(x^{2^j}\);
  • \(f_{j+k}(x)-f_j(x)\equiv 0\pmod{x^{2^j}}\) for all \(k\).

Substituting \(f_{j+1}(x)\) and \(f_j(x)\) into the expression above gives:

\[ G(f_{j+1}(x)) = G(f_j(x)) + 2f_j(f_{j+1}(x)-f_j(x)) + (f_{j+1}(x)-f_{j}(x))^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{x^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Clearly \(f_{j+1}(x)-f_j(x)\) must be a multiple of \(x^{2^j}\). Thus we get

\[ f_{j+1}(x) \equiv f_j(x) - \frac{f_j^2(x)-h(x)}{2f_j(x)} \equiv \frac{f_j(x)^2 + h(x)}{2f_j(x)} \pmod{x^{2^{j+1}}} \]

If \(f_j(x)\) exists, then \(2f_j(x)\) is not divisible by \(x\) (it has a constant term), so it necessarily has an inverse modulo \(x^{2^{j+1}}\). Therefore the sequence \(f_0,f_1\ldots,f_j\) exists if and only if \(f_0\) exists. A complex polynomial \(h(x)\) not divisible by \(x\) always has a square root modulo \(x\), because \(h(x)\) modulo \(x\) is just an ordinary nonzero complex number, which always has two square roots. So this algorithm can be applied to every \(h(x)\) with a constant term.

Taking \(h(x)=x+1\) as an example, the computation goes as follows:

  • \(f_0(x)=1\),\(f_1(x)=\dfrac{1^2+x+1}{2\times 1}\mod x^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}x+1\),\(f_2(x)=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)^2+x+1}{2\times \left(\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)}\mod x^4 = \dfrac{1}{16}x^3-\dfrac{1}{8}x^2+\dfrac{1}{2}x+1\),\(\ldots\)
  • \(f_0(x)=-1\),\(f_1(x)=\dfrac{(-1)^2+x+1}{2\times (-1)}\mod x^2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}x-1\),\(\ldots\) (equal to the negation of the previous one)

One can verify that both are correct sequences of modular square-root polynomials.

Square root of an integer modulo a prime power

The Newton iteration algorithm can also be carried over to integers modulo a power of a prime. Suppose \(h\) is a "convenient" integer not divisible by 3. ("Convenient" means "a solution necessarily exists"; the exact condition is given later.) Suppose we want to compute the square root \(f\) of \(h\) modulo \(3^n\). We have the equation:

\[ G\left(f\right) = f^2-h \equiv 0\pmod{3^{n}} \]

Taylor-expanding \(G\) gives:

\[ G(f) = \sum_{i=0}^{+\infty}\frac{G^{\left(i\right)}\left(f_{0}\right)}{i!}\left(f-f_{0}\right)^{i} = G(f_0) + 2f_0(f-f_0) + (f-f_0)^2 \]

We compute by doubling. Suppose the intermediate results obtained in the doubling are \(f_0, f_1, \ldots, f_j\), or, rigorously, \(f_j\) is an integer satisfying \(G(f_j)\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2^j}}\) which, for uniqueness, also satisfies the following two conditions:

  • \(0 < f_{j} < 3^{2^j}\);
  • \(f_{j+k}-f_j\equiv 0\pmod{3^{2^j}}\) for all \(k\).

Substituting \(f_{j+1}\) and \(f_j\) into the expression above gives:

\[ G(f_{j+1}) = G(f_j) + 2f_j(f_{j+1}-f_j) + (f_{j+1}-f_{j})^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{3^{2^{j+1}}} \]

Clearly \(f_{j+1}-f_j\) must be a multiple of \(3^{2^j}\). Thus we get

\[ f_{j+1} \equiv f_j - \frac{f_j^2-h}{2f_j} \equiv \frac{f_j^2 + h}{2f_j} \pmod{3^{2^{j+1}}} \]

If \(f_j\) exists, then \(2f_j\) is not divisible by \(3\), so it necessarily has an inverse modulo \(3^{2^{j+1}}\). Therefore the sequence \(f_0,f_1\ldots,f_j\) exists if and only if \(f_0\) exists. An integer \(h\) not divisible by 3 either has no square root modulo \(3\) or has two. So the only condition for the whole algorithm to work is that \(h\) has a square root modulo \(3\).

Here we take \(h=46\) as a concrete computation example.

  • \(f_0=1\),\(f_1=\dfrac{1^2+46}{2\times 1}\mod 9 = 1\),\(f_2=\dfrac{1^2+46}{2\times 1}\mod 81 = 64\),\(f_3=\dfrac{64^2+46}{2\times 64}\mod 6561 = 955\),\(\ldots\)
  • \(f_0=2\),\(f_1=\dfrac{2^2+46}{2\times 2}\mod 9 = 8\),\(f_2=\dfrac{8^2+46}{2\times 8}\mod 81 = 17\),\(f_3=\dfrac{17^2+46}{2\times 17}\mod 6561 = 5606\),\(\ldots\) (equal to the negation of the previous one)

One can verify that both are correct sequences of modular square roots.

Algebraic proof

This section extends the preceding text: in the language of abstract algebra we prove that as long as \(f\) satisfies the initial-solution condition, Newton's method gives a solution for every \(n\), and that all solutions can be obtained.

Proof that a solution exists

Lemma 1

Let an integral domain \(R\) have a polynomial or formal power series \(f(X) = \sum_{i\geq 0}a_iX^i\) and \(r,p\in R\) such that \(f(r)\in Rp\) (that is, \(r\) is a root of \(f(X)\) modulo \(p\)) and \(f'(r)\in R\) is invertible modulo \(p\). Here \(f'(X) := \sum_{i\geq 0}(i+1)a_{i+1}X^i\) is the formal derivative of \(f(X)\). Then \(f\left(r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\right) \equiv 0\pmod {p^2}\).

Proof

For every \(s\in R\),

\[ \begin{aligned} f(r+sp) &= \sum_{i\geq 0}a_i(r+sp)^i \\ &= \sum_{i\geq 0}a_ir^i + sp\sum_{i\geq 1}ia_ir^{i-1} + s^2p^2\left(\ldots\right) \\ &= f(r) + spf'(r) + s^2p^2\left(\frac{f''(r)}{2!} + \cdots\right), \end{aligned} \]

so

\[ f(r+sp) \in Rp^2 \iff f(r)+f'(r)sp \in Rp^2 \]

Since \(f(r)\in Rp\) and \(f'(r)\) is invertible, it suffices to take \(sp = -\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\), where \(\dfrac{1}{f'(r)}\) is the inverse modulo \(p^2\). Because \(f'(r)\) is invertible modulo \(p\), it necessarily also has an inverse modulo \(p^2\): let \(a,b,c\in R\) be such that \(af'(r) = bp+1\) and \(f(r)=cp\); then \(\left(a^2f'(r)-2\right)f'(r) = b^2p^2+1\), so we can take \(s=c(2-a^2f'(r))\).

For the polynomial ring \(k[X]\) over a field \(k\), let \(G(X, Y)\in k[X, Y]\) and \(f_n\in k[X]\) be such that \(G(X, f_n(X))\in k[X]X^n\); then applying Lemma 1 gives

\[ G\left(X, f_n(X) - \frac{G(X, f_n(X))}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n(X))} \right)\equiv 0 \pmod {X^{2n}} \]

For the initial condition of the doubling it suffices to have \(f_1\in k\) such that \(G(X, f_1)\equiv 0\pmod X\) and \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_1)\not\equiv 0\pmod X\). The latter condition guarantees that \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}\) has a nonzero constant term, and since \(X\left| \dfrac{G(X, f_n(X))}{\frac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n(X))} \right.\), for every \(n\) the value \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial Y}(X, f_n)\) is always invertible modulo \(X^n\), which satisfies the condition for the next iteration.

Proof that all solutions are obtained

Lemma 2

Let \(R\) be a UFD, with \(f,r,p\) defined as in Lemma 1. Then the \(r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)}\) given by Lemma 1 is the unique value of \(x\) modulo \(p^{2}\) satisfying the following two conditions:

  • \(f(x)\in Rp^{2}\)
  • \(x-r\in Rp\)

that is,

\[ \forall x\in R,\qquad p^2\mid f(x)\wedge p\mid (x-r) \implies x\equiv r-\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)} \pmod {p^2} \]
Proof

Let \(s = -\dfrac{f(r)}{f'(r)p}\) and \(u = r+sp\); Lemma 1 guarantees that \(u\) satisfies both conditions and that \(f(r) + f'(r)sp \in Rp^{2}\). Let \(v\) be a value satisfying the above conditions; then \(v = r+tp\) and \(f(r) + f'(r)tp \in Rp^{2}\). Hence \(f'(r)(t-s)p\in Rp^{2}\) and \(v-u\in Rp^{2}\).

Newton's method is guaranteed to obtain all solutions modulo \(X^{2^n}\). Suppose \(G(X, h)\equiv 0\pmod {X^{2^n}}\); let \(h_{2^i} := h\pmod {X^{2^i}}\), then take \(f_1 = h_1\) and apply Newton's method; by Lemma 2 we get \(f_{2^i} \equiv h_{2^i}\pmod {X^{2^i}}\), so necessarily \(f_{2^n} = h\).

The argument above also shows that when \(\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial y}(0, y)\) is always invertible, the number of solutions of \(G(X, f)\equiv 0\pmod {X^n}\) equals the number of solutions of \(G(0, f)\equiv 0\pmod X\). This conclusion is not trivial. See the following example.

An example where Newton's method fails and the number of solutions grows with the degree

Modulo \(X\) the only square root of \(X^2\) is \(0\), but modulo \(X^4\) the square roots of \(X^2\) are \(X, -X, X^3+X, \ldots\)
