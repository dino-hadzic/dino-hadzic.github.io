Pomak polinoma

Pomak polinoma (Taylor shift) jednostavan je poseban slučaj kompozicije polinoma: zadani su koeficijenti polinoma \(f(x)=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_ix^i\) i konstanta \(c\), a treba odrediti koeficijente od \(f(x+c)\), tj. \(f(x)\mapsto f(x+c)\).

Metoda podjele (divide and conquer)

Neka je

\[ f(x)=f_0(x)+x^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}f_1(x) \]

Tada je

\[ f(x+c)=f_0(x+c)+(x+c)^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}f_1(x+c) \]

Koeficijenti od \((x+c)^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}\) binomni su koeficijenti, pa je

\[ T(n)=2T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log^2 n) \]

pri čemu je \(O(n\log n)\) vrijeme množenja polinoma.

Metoda Taylorove formule

Primijenimo li na \(f(x)\) Taylorovu formulu u točki \(c\), imamo

\[ f(x)=f(c)+\frac{f'(c)}{1!}(x-c)+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}(x-c)^2+\cdots +\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}(x-c)^n \]

Tada je

\[ f(x+c)=f(c)+\frac{f'(c)}{1!}x+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}x^2+\cdots +\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}x^n \]

Uočimo da za \(t\geq 0\) vrijedi

\[ \begin{aligned} t!\lbrack x^t\rbrack f(x+c)&=f^{(t)}(c)\\ &=\sum _ {i=t}^nf_ii!\frac{c^{i-t}}{(i-t)!}\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t}f _ {i+t}(i+t)!\frac{c^i}{i!} \end{aligned} \]

Neka je

\[ \begin{aligned} A_0(x)&=\sum _ {i=0}^nf _ {n-i}(n-i)!x^i\\ B_0(x)&=\sum _ {i=0}^n\frac{c^i}{i!}x^i \end{aligned} \]

Tada je

\[ \begin{aligned} \lbrack x^{n-t}\rbrack (A_0(x)B_0(x))&=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t} (\lbrack x^{n-t-i}\rbrack A_0(x))(\lbrack x^i\rbrack B_0(x))\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t}f _ {i+t}(i+t)!\frac{c^i}{i!}\\ &=t!\lbrack x^t\rbrack f(x+c) \end{aligned} \]

Metoda binomnog teorema

Promotrimo binomni teorem \(\displaystyle (a+b)^n=\sum _ {i=0}^n\binom{n}{i}a^ib^{n-i}\); tada je

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x+c)&=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_i(x+c)^i\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_i\left(\sum _ {j=0}^i\binom{i}{j}x^jc^{i-j}\right)\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_ii!\left(\sum _ {j=0}^i\frac{x^j}{j!}\frac{c^{i-j}}{(i-j)!}\right)\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^n\frac{x^i}{i!}\left(\sum _ {j=i}^{n}f_jj!\frac{c^{j-i}}{(j-i)!}\right) \end{aligned} \]

Dobiveni rezultat isti je kao kod gornje metode.

Pomak uzastopnih vrijednosti u točkama

Primjer zadatka LOJ 166 拉格朗日插值 2 (Lagrangeova interpolacija 2)

Zadane su uzastopne vrijednosti \(f(0),f(1),\dots ,f(n)\) polinoma \(f\) stupnja najviše \(n\); modulo \(998244353\) izračunajte \(f(c),f(c+1),\dots ,f(c+n)\), pri čemu je \(1\leq n\leq 10^5,n < m\leq 10^8\).

Metoda Lagrangeove interpolacijske formule

Promotrimo Lagrangeovu interpolacijsku formulu

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x)&=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}f(i)\prod _ {0\leq j\leq n\,\land \,j\neq i}\frac{x-j}{i-j}\\ &=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}f(i)\frac{x!}{(x-n-1)!(x-i)}\frac{(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}\\ &=\frac{x!}{(x-n-1)!}\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}\frac{f(i)}{(x-i)}\frac{(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!} \end{aligned} \]

Gornji izraz jest oblika konvolucije, ali ne jamči da je u nazivniku \(x-i\neq 0\), pa u nastavku razmatramo samo slučaj \(c > n\); ostali slučajevi (npr. kada se radi s koeficijentima modulo prost broj, treba izbjeći da se u nazivnicima koeficijenata od \(B_0(x)\) pojavi nula) rješavaju se razlikovanjem slučajeva. Neka je

\[ \begin{aligned} A_0(x)&=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}\frac{f(i)(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}x^i\\ B_0(x)&=\sum _ {i\geq 0}\frac{1}{c-n+i}x^i \end{aligned} \]

Tada za \(t\geq 0\) vrijedi

\[ \begin{aligned} \lbrack x^{n+t}\rbrack (A_0(x)B_0(x))&=\sum _ {i=0}^{n+t}(\lbrack x^i\rbrack A_0(x))(\lbrack x^{n+t-i}\rbrack B_0(x))\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n}\frac{f(i)(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}\frac{1}{c+t-i}\\ &=\frac{(c+t-n-1)!}{(c+t)!}f(c+t) \end{aligned} \]

U implementaciji od \(B_0(x)\) uzimamo samo potreban odsječak, čime možemo dobiti i više vrijednosti, a može se iskoristiti i ciklička konvolucija.

Malo izmijenimo problem: pretpostavimo da su za neki \(d\) zadane vrijednosti \(f(d),f(d+k),\dots ,f(d+nk)\); tada možemo izračunati \(f(c+d),f(c+d+k),\dots ,f(c+d+nk)\), što promatramo kao pomak vrijednosti \(g(0),g(1),\dots ,g(n)\) polinoma \(g(x)=f(d+kx)\) u \(g(c/k),g(c/k+1),\dots ,g(c/k+n)\).

Lagrangeova interpolacijska formula daje i način da se jedna vrijednost izračuna u linearnom vremenu održavanjem nekoliko prefiksnih i sufiksnih produkata.

Primjene

Stirlingovi brojevi prve vrste (bez predznaka) u istom retku

Primjer zadatka P5408 第一类斯特林数·行 (Stirlingovi brojevi prve vrste – redak)

Modulo prost broj \(167772161\) izračunajte \(\displaystyle {n\brack 0},{n\brack 1},\dots ,{n\brack n}\), pri čemu je \(1\leq n< 262144\).

Promotrimo

\[ x^{\overline{n}}=\sum _ {i=0}^n{n\brack i}x^i,\quad n\geq 0 \]

pri čemu je \(x^{\overline{n}}=x\cdot (x+1)\cdots (x+n-1)\) rastuća faktorijelna potencija (rising factorial). Neka je \(f_n(x)=x^{\overline{n}}\); tada je

\[ f_{2n}(x)=x^{\overline{n}}\cdot (x+n)^{\overline{n}}=f_n(x)f_n(x+n) \]

Pomakom polinoma možemo u \(O(n\log n)\) odrediti \(f_n(x+n)\), pa se problem smanjuje na polovinu izvornoga, tj. na određivanje koeficijenata od \(f_n(x)\); dakle

\[ T(n)=T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log n) \]

Faktorijel modulo prost broj

Primjer zadatka P5282【模板】快速阶乘算法 (Predložak: brzi algoritam za faktorijel)

Izračunajte \(n!\bmod p\), pri čemu je \(p\) prost i \(1\leq n< p\leq 2^{31}-1\).

Neka je \(v=\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor\) i \(g(x)=\prod _ {i=1}^v(x+i)\); tada je

\[ n!\equiv \left(\prod _ {i=0}^{v-1}g(iv)\right)\cdot \prod _ {i=v^2+1}^n i\pmod{p} \]

pri čemu se \(\prod _ {i=v^2+1}^n i\) može izračunati u vremenu \(O(\sqrt{n})\); želimo brzo izračunati prvi dio gornjeg izraza.

Evaluacija polinoma u više točaka

Koeficijenti od \(g(x)\) mogu se gornjim algoritmom pomaka polinoma dobiti u vremenu \(O(n\log n)\), ali evaluacija u više točaka \(g(0),g(v),g(2v),\dots ,g(v^2-v)\) zahtijeva vrijeme \(O(\sqrt{n}\log ^2n)\).

Pomak uzastopnih vrijednosti u točkama

Neka je \(g_d(x)=\prod _ {i=1}^d(x+i)\); pomoću \(d+1\) vrijednosti \(g_d(0),g_d(v),\dots ,g_d(dv)\) jednoznačno možemo odrediti taj polinom stupnja \(d\), a k tome je

\[ g _ {2d}(x)=g_d(x)g_d(x+d) \]

pa je \(2d+1\) vrijednosti dovoljno da se jednoznačno odredi \(g _ {2d}(x)\). Stoga pomakom uzastopnih vrijednosti izračunamo \(g_d((d+1)v),g_d((d+2)v),\dots ,g_d(2dv)\) (tj. pomaknemo vrijednosti \(h(0),h(1),\dots ,h(d)\) polinoma \(h(x)=g_d(vx)\) u \(h(d+1),h(d+2),\dots ,h(2d)\)) i \(g_d(d),g_d(v+d),\dots ,g_d(2dv+d)\) (tj. pomaknemo vrijednosti \(h(0),h(1),\dots ,h(d)\) polinoma \(h(x)=g_d(vx)\) u \(h(d/v),h(d/v+1),h(d/v+2),\dots ,h(d/v+2d)\)), a zatim množenjem odgovarajućih vrijednosti dobivamo \(g _ {2d}(0),g _ {2d}(v),\dots ,g _ {2d}(2dv)\).

Da bismo iz \(g_d(0),g_d(v),\dots ,g_d(dv)\) izračunali \(g _ {d+1}(0),g _ {d+1}(v),\dots ,g _ {d+1}(dv),g _ {d+1}((d+1)v)\), promotrimo

\[ g _ {d+1}(x)=(x+d+1)\cdot g_d(x) \]

Za jednu dodatnu vrijednost dovoljan je algoritam linearnog vremena. Dakle, na početku održavamo \(g_1(0)=1,g_1(v)=v+1\), a zatim pomakom uzastopnih vrijednosti udvostručavanjem održavamo te vrijednosti; imamo

\[ T(n)=T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log n) \]

a treba nam samo oko \(\sqrt{n}\) vrijednosti, pa je vremenska složenost \(O(\sqrt{n}\log n)\).

Prefiksne sume binomnih koeficijenata modulo prost broj

Primjer zadatka LOJ 6386 组合数前缀和 (Prefiksna suma binomnih koeficijenata)

Izračunajte \(\displaystyle \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i}\bmod 998244353\), pri čemu je \(0\leq m\leq n\leq 9\times 10^8\).

Opišemo li korak rekurzije \(n!=n\cdot (n-1)!\) matricom, imamo

\[ \begin{bmatrix} n! \end{bmatrix} =\left( \prod _ {i=0}^{n-1} \begin{bmatrix}i+1\end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Slično se rekurzija za prefiksnu sumu binomnih koeficijenata može opisati kao

\[ \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m+1}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} (n-m)/(m+1)&0\\ 1&1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^{m-1}\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix} \]

Pazeći na redoslijed množenja matrica, imamo

\[ \begin{aligned} \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m+1}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix} &=\left( \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} (n-i)/(i+1)&0\\1&1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \frac{1}{(m+1)!} \left( \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

Neka je \(v=\lfloor\sqrt{m}\rfloor\); održavamo vrijednosti matrice

\[ \begin{aligned} M _ d(x)&= \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x)&h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

u točkama \(M _ d(0),M _ d(v),\dots ,M_d(dv)\), tj. \(f_d(0),f_d(v),\dots ,f_d(dv)\), \(h_d(0),\dots ,h_d(dv)\) i \(g_d(0),\dots ,g_d(dv)\), a k tome je

\[ \begin{aligned} M _ {2d}(x)&= \prod _ {i=1}^{2d} \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \left( \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x-d+n+1-i&0\\ x+d+i&x+d+i \end{bmatrix} \right) \left( \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix} \right) \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x+d)&0\\ g_d(x+d)&h_d(x+d) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x)&h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x+d)f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x+d)f_d(x)+h_d(x+d)g_d(x)&h_d(x+d)h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

pri čemu je element u donjem desnom kutu matrice upravo ono što održavamo u algoritmu za faktorijel; tada je

\[ \begin{aligned} \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix}= \left( \prod _ {i=(k+1)v}^m \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\ i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} f_v(kv)&0\\ g_v(kv)&h_v(kv) \end{bmatrix} \cdots \begin{bmatrix} f_v(0)&0\\ g_v(0)&h_v(0) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

što se može izračunati u vremenu \(O(\sqrt m\log m)\).

Harmonijski brojevi modulo prost broj

Primjer zadatka P5702 调和级数求和 (Suma harmonijskog reda)

Izračunajte \(\sum _ {i=1}^ni^{-1}\bmod p\), pri čemu je \(p\) prost i \(1\leq n< p< 2^{30}\).

Označimo \(H_n=\sum _ {k=1}^nk^{-1}\); jedan korak rekurzije glasi

\[ \begin{bmatrix} (n+1)!\\(n+1)!H _ {n+1} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} n+1&0\\1&n+1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} n!\\n!H_n \end{bmatrix} \]

Tada je

\[ \begin{bmatrix} {n+1\brack 1}\\{n+1\brack 2} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} n!\\n!H_n \end{bmatrix}= \left( \prod _ {i=0}^{n-1} \begin{bmatrix} i+1&0\\1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix} \]

Ovdje su \(\displaystyle {n+1\brack 1}\) i \(\displaystyle {n+1\brack 2}\) Stirlingovi brojevi prve vrste bez predznaka. Metoda održavanja vrijednosti matrice u točkama ista je kao gore.

Rekurzija s polinomnim koeficijentima

Kakav bismo algoritam, slično kao u gornjem primjeru brzog algoritma za faktorijel, željeli dobiti u općenitijem slučaju?

Primjer zadatka P6115【模板】整式递推 (Predložak: rekurzija s polinomnim koeficijentima)

Niz \(a\) zadovoljava \(\forall n\ge m,\sum_{k=0}^ma_{n-k}P_k(n)=0\), pri čemu su \(P_k\) polinomi stupnja najviše \(d\).
Zadani su koeficijenti svih \(P_k\) te \(a_0,a_1,\dots,a_{m-1}\); izračunajte \(a_n\). Rezultat je modulo \(998244353\). \(n\le6\times10^8\), \(1\le m,d\le7\), vremensko ograničenje \(7s\).

Da bismo sustavnije opisali konstrukciju matrica iz gornjih primjera, uvodimo pojam \(\lambda\)-matrice.

Za implementaciju rekurzije s polinomnim koeficijentima trebamo uočiti da u brzom algoritmu za faktorijel vrijednosti koje održavamo zapravo nisu \(n!\), nego \(\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}(aT+i)\), tj. odnos (množitelj) između para vrijednosti.

Budući da red \(m\) rekurzije s polinomnim koeficijentima više nije \(1\), ne možemo izravno održavati množitelj između para brojeva; umjesto toga održavamo linearnu transformaciju između para \(m\)-dimenzionalnih vektora, tj. matricu \(m\times m\), pri čemu svaki element matrice odgovara vrijednosti nekog polinoma u točki.

Lako se vidi da za opći problem izračuna dalekog koeficijenta rekurzije s polinomnim koeficijentima možemo konstruirati

\[ -{\frac{1}{P_0(n)}}\begin{bmatrix}P_1(n)&P_2(n)&P_3(n)&\cdots&P_{m-1}(n)&P_m(n)\\-P_0(n)\\&-P_0(n)\\&&-P_0(n)\\&&&\ddots\\&&&&-P_0(n)\\\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}a_{n-1}\\a_{n-2}\\a_{n-3}\\\vdots\\a_{n-m+1}\\a_{n-m}\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}a_n\\a_{n-1}\\a_{n-2}\\\vdots\\a_{n-m+2}\\a_{n-m+1}\end{bmatrix} \]

Neka je

\[ B(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix} P_1(\lambda)&P_2(\lambda)&P_3(\lambda)&\cdots&P_{m-1}(\lambda)&P_m(\lambda)\\ -P_0(\lambda)\\ &-P_0(\lambda)\\ &&-P_0(\lambda)\\ &&&\ddots\\ &&&&-P_0(\lambda)\\ \end{bmatrix} \]

Zanemarimo zasad faktor \(-\frac1{P_0(n)}\) sprijeda; sada želimo održavati veličine oblika \(\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}B(aT+m+i)\), pri čemu se množi zdesna nalijevo.

Lako se vidi da je \(B_T(\lambda)=\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}B(\lambda+i)\) \(\lambda\)-matrica čiji elementi imaju stupanj najviše \(dT\), pa ju je dovoljno održavati pomoću \(dT+1\) vrijednosti.

Stoga održavamo vrijednosti u točkama tih \(\lambda\)-matrica: \(B_T(m)\), \(B_T(m+T)\), \(B_T(m+2T)\), \(\dots\), \(B_T(m+(dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(m+dT^2)\), a zatim, slično kao u brzom algoritmu za faktorijel, izravno radimo pomak vrijednosti polinoma u točkama i udvostručavanje.

Konkretno, da bismo \(t=\log_2T\) povećali za \(1\), radimo sljedeće:

  1. U vremenu \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) dobijemo \(B_T(p+dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(2dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT)dT)\).
  2. U vremenu \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) dobijemo \(B_T(p+2dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(3dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT)dT)\).
  3. U vremenu \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) dobijemo \(B_T(p+3dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(4dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(4dT)dT)\).
  4. Izračunamo \(B_{2T}(v)=B_{T}(v+T)B_{T}(v)\).

U svakom krugu na pomak trošimo složenost \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\); istodobno u svakom krugu radimo samo \(\Theta(dT)\) množenja matrica, čija se složenost može uzeti kao \(O(m^3dT)\).

Na kraju, dovoljno je doći do \(T\ge\sqrt{n/d}\).

Ranije spomenuti faktor \(-\frac1{P_0(n)}\) rješava se na sličan način.

Tako je složenost pretprocesiranja \(\Theta(\sqrt{nd}(m^3+m^2\log(nd)))\).

Što se tiče upita, trebamo samo \(\Theta(n/T)\) množenja vektora s matricom i \(O(T)\) izravnih prijelaza.

Lako se vidi da taj dio računa nije usko grlo složenosti.

Stoga je ukupna složenost algoritma \(\Theta(\sqrt{nd}(m^3+m^2\log(nd)))\).

Pri implementaciji možemo trikom cikličke konvolucije smanjiti konstantu NTT-a.

U praksi najčešće izdvajamo daleki koeficijent poznate D-konačne (diferencijalno konačne) funkcije izvodnice, pa su \(m,d\) konstante, čime postižemo izračun dalekog koeficijenta u \(\Theta(\sqrt n\log n)\).

Polynomial shift

Polynomial shift (Taylor shift) is a simple special case of polynomial composition: given the coefficients of \(f(x)=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_ix^i\) and a constant \(c\), find the coefficients of \(f(x+c)\), i.e. \(f(x)\mapsto f(x+c)\).

Divide and conquer method

Let

\[ f(x)=f_0(x)+x^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}f_1(x) \]

Then

\[ f(x+c)=f_0(x+c)+(x+c)^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}f_1(x+c) \]

The coefficients of \((x+c)^{\left\lfloor n/2\right\rfloor}\) are binomial coefficients, so

\[ T(n)=2T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log^2 n) \]

where \(O(n\log n)\) is the time of polynomial multiplication.

Taylor's formula method

Applying Taylor's formula to \(f(x)\) at \(c\), we have

\[ f(x)=f(c)+\frac{f'(c)}{1!}(x-c)+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}(x-c)^2+\cdots +\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}(x-c)^n \]

Then

\[ f(x+c)=f(c)+\frac{f'(c)}{1!}x+\frac{f''(c)}{2!}x^2+\cdots +\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}x^n \]

Observe that for \(t\geq 0\) we have

\[ \begin{aligned} t!\lbrack x^t\rbrack f(x+c)&=f^{(t)}(c)\\ &=\sum _ {i=t}^nf_ii!\frac{c^{i-t}}{(i-t)!}\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t}f _ {i+t}(i+t)!\frac{c^i}{i!} \end{aligned} \]

Let

\[ \begin{aligned} A_0(x)&=\sum _ {i=0}^nf _ {n-i}(n-i)!x^i\\ B_0(x)&=\sum _ {i=0}^n\frac{c^i}{i!}x^i \end{aligned} \]

Then

\[ \begin{aligned} \lbrack x^{n-t}\rbrack (A_0(x)B_0(x))&=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t} (\lbrack x^{n-t-i}\rbrack A_0(x))(\lbrack x^i\rbrack B_0(x))\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n-t}f _ {i+t}(i+t)!\frac{c^i}{i!}\\ &=t!\lbrack x^t\rbrack f(x+c) \end{aligned} \]

Binomial theorem method

Consider the binomial theorem \(\displaystyle (a+b)^n=\sum _ {i=0}^n\binom{n}{i}a^ib^{n-i}\); then

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x+c)&=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_i(x+c)^i\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_i\left(\sum _ {j=0}^i\binom{i}{j}x^jc^{i-j}\right)\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^nf_ii!\left(\sum _ {j=0}^i\frac{x^j}{j!}\frac{c^{i-j}}{(i-j)!}\right)\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^n\frac{x^i}{i!}\left(\sum _ {j=i}^{n}f_jj!\frac{c^{j-i}}{(j-i)!}\right) \end{aligned} \]

The result is the same as with the method above.

Shift of consecutive point values

Example problem LOJ 166 拉格朗日插值 2 (Lagrange Interpolation 2)

Given the consecutive values \(f(0),f(1),\dots ,f(n)\) of a polynomial \(f\) of degree at most \(n\), compute \(f(c),f(c+1),\dots ,f(c+n)\) modulo \(998244353\), where \(1\leq n\leq 10^5,n < m\leq 10^8\).

Lagrange interpolation formula method

Consider the Lagrange interpolation formula

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x)&=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}f(i)\prod _ {0\leq j\leq n\,\land \,j\neq i}\frac{x-j}{i-j}\\ &=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}f(i)\frac{x!}{(x-n-1)!(x-i)}\frac{(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}\\ &=\frac{x!}{(x-n-1)!}\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}\frac{f(i)}{(x-i)}\frac{(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!} \end{aligned} \]

Although the expression above has the form of a convolution, it does not guarantee \(x-i\neq 0\) in the denominator, so below we only consider the case \(c > n\); the other cases (e.g. when the coefficients are taken modulo a prime, one must avoid zeros in the denominators of the coefficients of \(B_0(x)\)) can be handled by case analysis. Let

\[ \begin{aligned} A_0(x)&=\sum _ {0\leq i\leq n}\frac{f(i)(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}x^i\\ B_0(x)&=\sum _ {i\geq 0}\frac{1}{c-n+i}x^i \end{aligned} \]

Then for \(t\geq 0\) we have

\[ \begin{aligned} \lbrack x^{n+t}\rbrack (A_0(x)B_0(x))&=\sum _ {i=0}^{n+t}(\lbrack x^i\rbrack A_0(x))(\lbrack x^{n+t-i}\rbrack B_0(x))\\ &=\sum _ {i=0}^{n}\frac{f(i)(-1)^{n-i}}{i!(n-i)!}\frac{1}{c+t-i}\\ &=\frac{(c+t-n-1)!}{(c+t)!}f(c+t) \end{aligned} \]

In the implementation we truncate \(B_0(x)\) to the part we need, which can also yield more point values, and cyclic convolution can be used.

Slightly modifying the problem: suppose that for some \(d\) the given values are \(f(d),f(d+k),\dots ,f(d+nk)\); then we can compute \(f(c+d),f(c+d+k),\dots ,f(c+d+nk)\), viewed as shifting the point values \(g(0),g(1),\dots ,g(n)\) of \(g(x)=f(d+kx)\) to \(g(c/k),g(c/k+1),\dots ,g(c/k+n)\).

The Lagrange interpolation formula also gives a way to compute a single point value in linear time by maintaining some prefix and suffix products.

Applications

Unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind in one row

Example problem P5408 第一类斯特林数·行 (Stirling Numbers of the First Kind – Row)

Compute \(\displaystyle {n\brack 0},{n\brack 1},\dots ,{n\brack n}\) modulo the prime \(167772161\), where \(1\leq n< 262144\).

Consider

\[ x^{\overline{n}}=\sum _ {i=0}^n{n\brack i}x^i,\quad n\geq 0 \]

where \(x^{\overline{n}}=x\cdot (x+1)\cdots (x+n-1)\) is the rising factorial. Let \(f_n(x)=x^{\overline{n}}\); then

\[ f_{2n}(x)=x^{\overline{n}}\cdot (x+n)^{\overline{n}}=f_n(x)f_n(x+n) \]

Using polynomial shift we can find \(f_n(x+n)\) in \(O(n\log n)\), and the problem is reduced to half of the original, namely finding the coefficients of \(f_n(x)\); thus

\[ T(n)=T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log n) \]

Factorial modulo a prime

Example problem P5282【模板】快速阶乘算法 (Template: Fast Factorial Algorithm)

Compute \(n!\bmod p\), where \(p\) is prime and \(1\leq n< p\leq 2^{31}-1\).

Let \(v=\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor\) and \(g(x)=\prod _ {i=1}^v(x+i)\); then

\[ n!\equiv \left(\prod _ {i=0}^{v-1}g(iv)\right)\cdot \prod _ {i=v^2+1}^n i\pmod{p} \]

where \(\prod _ {i=v^2+1}^n i\) can be computed in \(O(\sqrt{n})\) time; we want to compute the first part of the expression above quickly.

Polynomial multipoint evaluation

The coefficients of \(g(x)\) can be obtained with the polynomial shift algorithm above in \(O(n\log n)\) time, but the multipoint evaluation of \(g(0),g(v),g(2v),\dots ,g(v^2-v)\) takes \(O(\sqrt{n}\log ^2n)\) time.

Shift of consecutive point values

Let \(g_d(x)=\prod _ {i=1}^d(x+i)\); with the \(d+1\) point values \(g_d(0),g_d(v),\dots ,g_d(dv)\) we can uniquely determine this polynomial of degree \(d\), and moreover

\[ g _ {2d}(x)=g_d(x)g_d(x+d) \]

so \(2d+1\) point values suffice to uniquely determine \(g _ {2d}(x)\). Hence we use the shift of consecutive point values to compute \(g_d((d+1)v),g_d((d+2)v),\dots ,g_d(2dv)\) (i.e. shift the point values \(h(0),h(1),\dots ,h(d)\) of \(h(x)=g_d(vx)\) to \(h(d+1),h(d+2),\dots ,h(2d)\)) and \(g_d(d),g_d(v+d),\dots ,g_d(2dv+d)\) (i.e. shift the point values \(h(0),h(1),\dots ,h(d)\) of \(h(x)=g_d(vx)\) to \(h(d/v),h(d/v+1),h(d/v+2),\dots ,h(d/v+2d)\)), and then multiply the corresponding point values to obtain \(g _ {2d}(0),g _ {2d}(v),\dots ,g _ {2d}(2dv)\).

To compute \(g _ {d+1}(0),g _ {d+1}(v),\dots ,g _ {d+1}(dv),g _ {d+1}((d+1)v)\) from \(g_d(0),g_d(v),\dots ,g_d(dv)\), consider

\[ g _ {d+1}(x)=(x+d+1)\cdot g_d(x) \]

The one extra point value can be obtained with a linear-time algorithm. So we start by maintaining \(g_1(0)=1,g_1(v)=v+1\) and then use the shift of consecutive point values to maintain these point values by doubling; we have

\[ T(n)=T(n/2)+O(n\log n)=O(n\log n) \]

and we only need about \(\sqrt{n}\) point values, so the time complexity is \(O(\sqrt{n}\log n)\).

Prefix sums of binomial coefficients modulo a prime

Example problem LOJ 6386 组合数前缀和 (Prefix Sum of Binomial Coefficients)

Compute \(\displaystyle \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i}\bmod 998244353\), where \(0\leq m\leq n\leq 9\times 10^8\).

Describing the recurrence step \(n!=n\cdot (n-1)!\) with a matrix, we have

\[ \begin{bmatrix} n! \end{bmatrix} =\left( \prod _ {i=0}^{n-1} \begin{bmatrix}i+1\end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Similarly, the recurrence for the prefix sum of binomial coefficients can be described as

\[ \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m+1}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} (n-m)/(m+1)&0\\ 1&1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^{m-1}\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix} \]

Paying attention to the order of matrix multiplication, we have

\[ \begin{aligned} \begin{bmatrix} \binom{n}{m+1}\\ \sum _ {i=0}^m\binom{n}{i} \end{bmatrix} &=\left( \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} (n-i)/(i+1)&0\\1&1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \frac{1}{(m+1)!} \left( \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

Let \(v=\lfloor\sqrt{m}\rfloor\); we maintain the point values of the matrix

\[ \begin{aligned} M _ d(x)&= \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x)&h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

namely \(M _ d(0),M _ d(v),\dots ,M_d(dv)\), i.e. \(f_d(0),f_d(v),\dots ,f_d(dv)\), \(h_d(0),\dots ,h_d(dv)\) and \(g_d(0),\dots ,g_d(dv)\); moreover

\[ \begin{aligned} M _ {2d}(x)&= \prod _ {i=1}^{2d} \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix}\\ &= \left( \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x-d+n+1-i&0\\ x+d+i&x+d+i \end{bmatrix} \right) \left( \prod _ {i=1}^d \begin{bmatrix} -x+n+1-i&0\\ x+i&x+i \end{bmatrix} \right) \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x+d)&0\\ g_d(x+d)&h_d(x+d) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x)&h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} f_d(x+d)f_d(x)&0\\ g_d(x+d)f_d(x)+h_d(x+d)g_d(x)&h_d(x+d)h_d(x) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

and the bottom-right element of the matrix is exactly what we maintain in the factorial algorithm; then

\[ \begin{aligned} \prod _ {i=0}^{m} \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix}= \left( \prod _ {i=(k+1)v}^m \begin{bmatrix} n-i&0\\ i+1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} f_v(kv)&0\\ g_v(kv)&h_v(kv) \end{bmatrix} \cdots \begin{bmatrix} f_v(0)&0\\ g_v(0)&h_v(0) \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]

which can be computed in \(O(\sqrt m\log m)\) time.

Harmonic numbers modulo a prime

Example problem P5702 调和级数求和 (Harmonic Series Sum)

Compute \(\sum _ {i=1}^ni^{-1}\bmod p\), where \(p\) is prime and \(1\leq n< p< 2^{30}\).

Let \(H_n=\sum _ {k=1}^nk^{-1}\); one recurrence step is

\[ \begin{bmatrix} (n+1)!\\(n+1)!H _ {n+1} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} n+1&0\\1&n+1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} n!\\n!H_n \end{bmatrix} \]

Then

\[ \begin{bmatrix} {n+1\brack 1}\\{n+1\brack 2} \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} n!\\n!H_n \end{bmatrix}= \left( \prod _ {i=0}^{n-1} \begin{bmatrix} i+1&0\\1&i+1 \end{bmatrix} \right) \begin{bmatrix} 1\\0 \end{bmatrix} \]

Here \(\displaystyle {n+1\brack 1}\) and \(\displaystyle {n+1\brack 2}\) are unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind. The method of maintaining the point values of the matrix is the same as above.

Recurrences with polynomial coefficients

In the more general case, similar to the fast factorial algorithm above, what kind of algorithm do we expect to obtain?

Example problem P6115【模板】整式递推 (Template: Recurrence with Polynomial Coefficients)

A sequence \(a\) satisfies \(\forall n\ge m,\sum_{k=0}^ma_{n-k}P_k(n)=0\), where the \(P_k\) are polynomials of degree at most \(d\).
Given the coefficients of all \(P_k\) and \(a_0,a_1,\dots,a_{m-1}\), compute \(a_n\). The answer is taken modulo \(998244353\). \(n\le6\times10^8\), \(1\le m,d\le7\), time limit \(7s\).

To describe the construction of the matrices in the examples above more systematically, we introduce the concept of a \(\lambda\)-matrix.

To implement recurrences with polynomial coefficients, we should note that in the fast factorial algorithm the point values we maintain are actually not \(n!\) but \(\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}(aT+i)\), i.e. the ratio between a pair of point values.

Since the order \(m\) of a recurrence with polynomial coefficients is no longer \(1\), we can no longer directly maintain the ratio between a pair of numbers; instead we maintain the linear transformation between a pair of \(m\)-dimensional vectors, i.e. an \(m\times m\) matrix, where each entry of the matrix corresponds to a point value of some polynomial.

It is easy to see that for the general problem of evaluating a far coefficient of a recurrence with polynomial coefficients we can construct

\[ -{\frac{1}{P_0(n)}}\begin{bmatrix}P_1(n)&P_2(n)&P_3(n)&\cdots&P_{m-1}(n)&P_m(n)\\-P_0(n)\\&-P_0(n)\\&&-P_0(n)\\&&&\ddots\\&&&&-P_0(n)\\\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}a_{n-1}\\a_{n-2}\\a_{n-3}\\\vdots\\a_{n-m+1}\\a_{n-m}\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}a_n\\a_{n-1}\\a_{n-2}\\\vdots\\a_{n-m+2}\\a_{n-m+1}\end{bmatrix} \]

Let

\[ B(\lambda)=\begin{bmatrix} P_1(\lambda)&P_2(\lambda)&P_3(\lambda)&\cdots&P_{m-1}(\lambda)&P_m(\lambda)\\ -P_0(\lambda)\\ &-P_0(\lambda)\\ &&-P_0(\lambda)\\ &&&\ddots\\ &&&&-P_0(\lambda)\\ \end{bmatrix} \]

Setting aside the leading factor \(-\frac1{P_0(n)}\) for now, we want to maintain quantities of the form \(\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}B(aT+m+i)\), where the multiplication goes from right to left.

It is easy to see that \(B_T(\lambda)=\prod_{i=0}^{T-1}B(\lambda+i)\) is a \(\lambda\)-matrix whose entries have degree at most \(dT\), so \(dT+1\) values suffice to maintain it.

Hence we maintain the point values of the \(\lambda\)-matrices \(B_T(m)\), \(B_T(m+T)\), \(B_T(m+2T)\), \(\dots\), \(B_T(m+(dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(m+dT^2)\), and then, similarly to the fast factorial algorithm, directly perform shifts of polynomial point values and doubling.

Specifically, to raise \(t=\log_2T\) by \(1\), we do the following:

  1. In \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) time obtain \(B_T(p+dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(2dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT)dT)\).
  2. In \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) time obtain \(B_T(p+2dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(2dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(3dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT)dT)\).
  3. In \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) time obtain \(B_T(p+3dT^2)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT+1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(3dT+2)T)\), \(\cdots\), \(B_T(p+(4dT-1)T)\), \(B_T(p+(4dT)dT)\).
  4. Compute \(B_{2T}(v)=B_{T}(v+T)B_{T}(v)\).

In each round we spend \(O(m^2dT\log(dT))\) on the shifts; at the same time, in each round we only perform \(\Theta(dT)\) matrix multiplications, whose complexity can be taken as \(O(m^3dT)\).

Finally, we only need to go up to \(T\ge\sqrt{n/d}\).

The factor \(-\frac1{P_0(n)}\) from before can be handled in a similar way.

Thus the preprocessing complexity is \(\Theta(\sqrt{nd}(m^3+m^2\log(nd)))\).

For the query, we only need \(\Theta(n/T)\) vector–matrix multiplications and \(O(T)\) brute-force transitions.

It is easy to see that this part of the computation is not the complexity bottleneck.

Therefore the total complexity of the algorithm is \(\Theta(\sqrt{nd}(m^3+m^2\log(nd)))\).

When coding, we can use the cyclic convolution trick to reduce the constant of the NTT.

In practice we usually extract a far coefficient of a known D-finite (differentiably finite) generating function, so \(m,d\) are constants, and we achieve far-coefficient evaluation in \(\Theta(\sqrt n\log n)\).
