Definicija

Kongruencija

Za prirodan broj \(m\) i polinom jedne varijable s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^n a_ix^i\), gdje je nepoznanica \(x\in\mathbf{Z}_m\), jednadžba oblika

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod m\tag{1} \]

naziva se kongruencija (congruence equation) jedne nepoznanice \(x\) po modulu \(m\).

Ako je \(a_n\not\equiv 0\pmod m\), gornja se jednadžba naziva kongruencijom \(n\)-tog stupnja.

Analogno se definira sustav kongruencija.

O kongruencijama prvog stupnja i njihovim sustavima vidi Linearne kongruencije i Kineski teorem o ostatcima.

U ovom se članku najprije proučavaju rješivost kongruencija i struktura skupa rješenja, a zatim se ukratko opisuju metode rješavanja kongruencija višeg stupnja.

Prema kineskom teoremu o ostatcima, rješavanje kongruencije po složenom modulu \(m\) može se svesti na rješavanje po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja. Stoga u nastavku predstavljamo samo teoriju kongruencija po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja i kongruencija po prostom modulu.

Kongruencije po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja

U nastavku pretpostavljamo da je modul \(m=p^e~(p\in\mathbf{P},~e\in\mathbf{Z}_{>1})\).

Uočimo: ako je \(x_0\) rješenje jednadžbe

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

tada je \(x_0\) rješenje jednadžbe

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^{e-1}} \]

To nas potiče da pokušamo iz rješenja po nižoj potenciji modula konstruirati rješenja po višoj potenciji. Vrijedi sljedeći teorem:

Teorem 1 (Henselova lema)

Za prost broj \(p\) i cijeli broj \(e>1\) uzmimo polinom s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^na_ix^i~(p^e\nmid a_n)\) i neka je \(f'(x)=\sum_{i=1}^nia_ix^{i-1}\) njegova derivacija. Neka je \(x_0\) rješenje jednadžbe

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^{e-1}}\tag{2} \]

Tada:

  1. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), postoji cijeli broj \(t\) takav da je

    \[ x=x_0+p^{e-1}t \tag{3} \]

    rješenje jednadžbe

    \[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \tag{4} \]

  2. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), tada su za \(t=0,1,\dots,p-1\) svi \(x\) određeni formulom \((3)\) rješenja jednadžbe \((4)\).

  3. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), tada se formulom \((3)\) ne može konstruirati rješenje jednadžbe \((4)\).
Dokaz

Pretpostavimo da je \((3)\) rješenje jednadžbe \((4)\), tj.

\[ f(x_0+p^{e-1}t)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

Sređivanjem dobivamo

\[ f(x_0)+p^{e-1}tf'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

pa je

\[ tf'(x_0)\equiv -\frac{f(x_0)}{p^{e-1}}\pmod p\tag{5} \]
  1. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), jednadžba \((5)\) po \(t\) ima jedinstveno rješenje \(t_0\); uvrštavanjem u \((3)\) provjerava se da je to rješenje jednadžbe \((4)\).
  2. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), svaki \(t\) zadovoljava jednadžbu \((5)\); uvrštavanjem u \((3)\) provjerava se da su svi oni rješenja jednadžbe \((4)\).
  3. Ako je \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), jednadžba \((5)\) nema rješenja, pa se formulom \((3)\) ne može konstruirati rješenje jednadžbe \((4)\).

Odatle slijedi korolar:

Korolar 1

Za \(p\), \(e\), \(f(x)\), \(x_0\) iz teorema 1:

  1. Ako je \(s\) rješenje jednadžbe \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f'(s)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), postoji \(x_s\in\mathbf{Z}_{p^e}\), \(x_s\equiv s\pmod p\), takav da je \(x_s\) rješenje jednadžbe \((4)\).
  2. Ako jednadžbe \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(f'(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) nemaju zajedničko rješenje, jednadžba \((4)\) i jednadžba \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) imaju jednak broj rješenja.

Time kongruencije po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja možemo svesti na slučaj kongruencija po prostom modulu.

Kongruencije po prostom modulu

U nastavku neka je \(p\in\mathbf{P}\), \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^na_ix^i\) polinom s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima, gdje je \(p\nmid a_n\), i \(x\in\mathbf{Z}_p\).

Teorem 2

Ako jednadžba

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{6} \]

ima \(k\) različitih rješenja \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k~(k\leq n)\), tada je

\[ f(x)\equiv g(x)\prod_{i=1}^k(x-x_i)\pmod p, \]

gdje je \(\deg g=n-k\) i \([x^{n-k}]g(x)=a_n\).

Dokaz

Matematičkom indukcijom po \(k\).

  • Za \(k=1\) dijeljenjem polinoma s ostatkom dobivamo \(f(x)=(x-x_1)g(x)+r\), gdje je \(r\in\mathbf{Z}\).

    Iz \(f(x_1)\equiv 0\pmod p\) slijedi \(r\equiv 0\pmod p\), pa je \(f(x)\equiv(x-x_1)g(x)\pmod p\).

  • Pretpostavimo da tvrdnja vrijedi za \(k-1\) (\(k>1\)) i neka sada \(f(x)\) ima \(k\) različitih rješenja \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k\); tada je \(f(x)\equiv(x-x_1)h(x)\pmod p\), pa vrijedi

    \[ (\forall i=2,3,\dots,k),~~0\equiv f(x_i)\equiv (x_i-x_1)h(x_i)\pmod p \]

    Stoga \(h(x)\) ima \(k-1\) različitih rješenja \(x_2,x_3,\dots,x_k\), pa po pretpostavci indukcije vrijedi

    \[ h(x)\equiv g(x)\prod_{i=2}^k(x-x_i)\pmod p \]

    gdje je \(\deg g=n-k\) i \([x^{n-k}]g(x)=a_n\).

    Time je tvrdnja dokazana.

Korolar 2

Za prost broj \(p\):

  • \((\forall x\in\mathbf{Z}),~~x^{p-1}-1 \equiv \prod_{i=1}^{p-1}(x-i)\pmod p\).
  • (Wilsonov teorem) \((p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p\).
Teorem 3 (Lagrangeov teorem)

Jednadžba \((6)\) ima najviše \(n\) različitih rješenja.

Dokaz

Pretpostavimo da \(f(x)\) ima \(n+1\) različitih rješenja \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{n+1}\); tada prema teoremu 2 za \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n\) vrijedi

\[ f(x)\equiv a_n\prod_{i=1}^n(x-x_i)\pmod p \]

Stavimo \(x=x_{n+1}\); tada je

\[ 0\equiv f(x_{n+1})\equiv a_n\prod_{i=1}^n(x_{n+1}-x_i)\pmod p \]

a desna strana očito nije višekratnik broja \(p\), pa je pretpostavka kontradiktorna.

Korolar 3

Ako kongruencija \(\sum_{i=0}^nb_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\) ima više od \(n\) rješenja, tada je

\[ (\forall i=0,1,\dots,n),~~p\mid b_i. \]
Teorem 4

Ako broj rješenja jednadžbe \((6)\) nije \(p\), tada nužno postoji polinom s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(r(x)\) s \(\deg r<p\) takav da \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i \(r(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) imaju isti skup rješenja.

Dokaz

Bez smanjenja općenitosti neka je \(n\geq p\); podijelimo \(f(x)\) s ostatkom:

\[ f(x)=g(x)\left(x^p-x\right)+r(x) \]

gdje je \(\deg r<p\).

Prema malom Fermatovu teoremu za svaki cijeli broj \(x\) vrijedi \(x^p\equiv x\pmod p\), pa

  • ako je \(r(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\), prema korolaru 2 \(f(x)\) ima \(p\) različitih rješenja;
  • ako je \(r(x)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), iz \(f(x)\equiv r(x)\pmod p\) slijedi da \(f(x)\) i \(r(x)\) imaju isti skup rješenja.

Pomoću ovog teorema možemo sniziti stupanj kongruencije.

Teorem 5

Neka je \(n\leq p\). Jednadžba

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{7} \]

ima \(n\) rješenja ako i samo ako postoje polinomi s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(q(x)\), \(r(x)~(\deg r < n)\) takvi da je

\[ x^p-x=f(x)q(x)+pr(x). \tag{8} \]
Dokaz

  • Nužnost: dijeljenjem polinoma znamo da postoje polinomi s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(q(x)\), \(r_1(x)~(\deg r_1 < n)\) takvi da je

    \[ x^p-x=f(x)q(x)+r_1(x). \]

    Ako jednadžba \((7)\) ima \(n\) rješenja, tada i \(r_1\equiv 0\pmod p\) ima tih istih \(n\) rješenja, pa prema korolaru 3 postoji polinom s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima \(r(x)\) takav da je \(r_1(x)=pr(x)\); time je tvrdnja dokazana.

  • Dovoljnost: ako vrijedi \((8)\), prema malom Fermatovu teoremu za svaki cijeli broj \(x\) vrijedi

    \[ 0\equiv x^p-x\equiv f(x)q(x)\pmod p. \]

    Dakle, jednadžba \(f(x)q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) ima \(p\) rješenja.

    Neka je \(s\) broj rješenja jednadžbe \((7)\); prema Lagrangeovu teoremu \(s\leq n\).

    Nadalje, budući da je \(\deg q=p-n\), prema Lagrangeovu teoremu broj rješenja jednadžbe \(q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) ne premašuje \(p-n\), a skup rješenja jednadžbe \(f(x)q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) unija je skupa rješenja jednadžbe \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) i skupa rješenja jednadžbe \(q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\), pa je \(s+(p-n)\geq p\), tj. \(s\geq n\).

    Stoga je \(s=n\).

Za polinome koji nisu normirani, budući da je \(\mathbf{Z}_p\) polje, mogu se svesti na normirane polinome, pa se teorem može primijeniti.

Teorem 6

Neka je \(n\mid p-1\) i \(p\nmid a\). Jednadžba

\[ x^n\equiv a\pmod p\tag{9} \]

ima rješenje ako i samo ako je

\[ a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p. \]

Štoviše, ako \((9)\) ima rješenje, broj rješenja iznosi \(n\).

Napomena

Konkretna struktura skupa rješenja jednadžbe \((9)\) opisana je u članku Ostatci k-tog reda.

Dokaz

  • Nužnost: ako jednadžba \((9)\) ima rješenje \(x_0\), tada je

    \[ a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv {\left(x_0^n\right)}^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p \]

  • Dovoljnost: ako je \(a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p\), tada je

    \[ \begin{aligned} x^p-x&=x\left(x^{p-1}-1\right)\\ &=x\left(\left(x^n\right)^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}+a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-1\right)\\ &=\left(x^n-a\right)P(x)+x\left(a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-1\right)\\ \end{aligned} \]

    gdje je \(P(x)\) neki polinom s cjelobrojnim koeficijentima, pa prema teoremu 5 jednadžba \((9)\) ima \(n\) rješenja.

Metode rješavanja kongruencija (i sustava) višeg stupnja

Najprije pomoću kineskog teorema o ostatcima rješavanje sustava kongruencija svodimo na rješavanje kongruencija, a rješavanje kongruencije po složenom modulu \(m\) na rješavanje kongruencija po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja. Zatim pomoću teorema 1 rješavanje kongruencije po modulu koji je potencija prostog broja svodimo na rješavanje kongruencije po prostom modulu.

Uzimajući u obzir teoreme o kongruencijama po prostom modulu, dovoljno je razmotriti rješavanje jednadžbe

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p \]

gdje je \(p\) prost broj, a \(n<p\).

Zamjenom \(x\) s \(x-\dfrac{a_{n-1}}{n}\) možemo ukloniti član \(x^{n-1}\), pa je dovoljno razmotriti rješavanje jednadžbe

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-2}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{10} \]

gdje je \(p\) prost broj, a \(n<p\).

  • Za \(n=1\) postupak je opisan u članku Linearne kongruencije.
  • Za \(n=2\) postupak je opisan u članku Kvadratni ostatci.

  • Ako se jednadžba \((10)\) može svesti na oblik

    \[ x^n\equiv a\pmod p, \]

    postupak je opisan u članku Ostatci k-tog reda.

Definition

Congruence equation

For a positive integer \(m\) and a univariate polynomial with integer coefficients \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^n a_ix^i\), where the unknown is \(x\in\mathbf{Z}_m\), an equation of the form

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod m\tag{1} \]

is called a univariate congruence equation in the unknown \(x\) modulo \(m\).

If \(a_n\not\equiv 0\pmod m\), the equation above is called a congruence equation of degree \(n\).

Systems of congruence equations are defined analogously.

For congruence equations of degree one and their systems, see Linear congruences and Chinese remainder theorem.

This article first studies the solvability of congruence equations and the structure of their solution sets, and then briefly introduces methods for solving higher-degree congruence equations.

By the Chinese remainder theorem, solving a congruence equation modulo a composite \(m\) can be reduced to solving it modulo prime powers. Therefore, below we only introduce the theory of congruence equations modulo a prime power and modulo a prime.

Congruences modulo a prime power

Below we assume that the modulus is \(m=p^e~(p\in\mathbf{P},~e\in\mathbf{Z}_{>1})\).

Note that if \(x_0\) is a solution of the equation

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

then \(x_0\) is a solution of the equation

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^{e-1}} \]

This inspires us to try to construct solutions modulo a higher power from solutions modulo a lower power. We have the following theorem:

Theorem 1 (Hensel's lemma)

For a prime \(p\) and an integer \(e>1\), take a polynomial with integer coefficients \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^na_ix^i~(p^e\nmid a_n)\), and let \(f'(x)=\sum_{i=1}^nia_ix^{i-1}\) be its derivative. Let \(x_0\) be a solution of the equation

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^{e-1}}\tag{2} \]

Then:

  1. If \(f'(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), there exists an integer \(t\) such that

    \[ x=x_0+p^{e-1}t \tag{3} \]

    is a solution of the equation

    \[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \tag{4} \]

  2. If \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), then for \(t=0,1,\dots,p-1\) all \(x\) determined by formula \((3)\) are solutions of equation \((4)\).

  3. If \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), then no solution of equation \((4)\) can be constructed by formula \((3)\).
Proof

Suppose that \((3)\) is a solution of equation \((4)\), i.e.

\[ f(x_0+p^{e-1}t)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

Rearranging, we get

\[ f(x_0)+p^{e-1}tf'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e} \]

hence

\[ tf'(x_0)\equiv -\frac{f(x_0)}{p^{e-1}}\pmod p\tag{5} \]
  1. If \(f'(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), equation \((5)\) in \(t\) has a unique solution \(t_0\); substituting into \((3)\), one verifies that it is a solution of equation \((4)\).
  2. If \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), every \(t\) satisfies equation \((5)\); substituting into \((3)\), one verifies that they are all solutions of equation \((4)\).
  3. If \(f'(x_0)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f(x_0)\not\equiv 0\pmod{p^e}\), equation \((5)\) has no solution, so no solution of equation \((4)\) can be constructed by formula \((3)\).

Hence we have the corollary:

Corollary 1

For \(p\), \(e\), \(f(x)\), \(x_0\) as in Theorem 1:

  1. If \(s\) is a solution of the equation \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f'(s)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), then there exists \(x_s\in\mathbf{Z}_{p^e}\), \(x_s\equiv s\pmod p\), such that \(x_s\) is a solution of equation \((4)\).
  2. If the equations \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(f'(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) have no common solution, then equation \((4)\) and the equation \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) have the same number of solutions.

Thus we can reduce congruence equations modulo a prime power to the case of congruence equations modulo a prime.

Congruences modulo a prime

Below, let \(p\in\mathbf{P}\), let \(f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^na_ix^i\) be a polynomial with integer coefficients where \(p\nmid a_n\), and let \(x\in\mathbf{Z}_p\).

Theorem 2

If the equation

\[ f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{6} \]

has \(k\) distinct solutions \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k~(k\leq n)\), then

\[ f(x)\equiv g(x)\prod_{i=1}^k(x-x_i)\pmod p, \]

where \(\deg g=n-k\) and \([x^{n-k}]g(x)=a_n\).

Proof

By mathematical induction on \(k\).

  • For \(k=1\), polynomial division with remainder gives \(f(x)=(x-x_1)g(x)+r\), where \(r\in\mathbf{Z}\).

    From \(f(x_1)\equiv 0\pmod p\) we get \(r\equiv 0\pmod p\), hence \(f(x)\equiv(x-x_1)g(x)\pmod p\).

  • Suppose the statement holds for \(k-1\) (\(k>1\)), and now let \(f(x)\) have \(k\) distinct solutions \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k\); then \(f(x)\equiv(x-x_1)h(x)\pmod p\), and hence

    \[ (\forall i=2,3,\dots,k),~~0\equiv f(x_i)\equiv (x_i-x_1)h(x_i)\pmod p \]

    Therefore \(h(x)\) has \(k-1\) distinct solutions \(x_2,x_3,\dots,x_k\), and by the induction hypothesis

    \[ h(x)\equiv g(x)\prod_{i=2}^k(x-x_i)\pmod p \]

    where \(\deg g=n-k\) and \([x^{n-k}]g(x)=a_n\).

    This proves the statement.

Corollary 2

For a prime \(p\):

  • \((\forall x\in\mathbf{Z}),~~x^{p-1}-1 \equiv \prod_{i=1}^{p-1}(x-i)\pmod p\).
  • (Wilson's theorem) \((p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p\).
Theorem 3 (Lagrange's theorem)

Equation \((6)\) has at most \(n\) distinct solutions.

Proof

Suppose \(f(x)\) has \(n+1\) distinct solutions \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{n+1}\); then by Theorem 2, for \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n\) we have

\[ f(x)\equiv a_n\prod_{i=1}^n(x-x_i)\pmod p \]

Let \(x=x_{n+1}\); then

\[ 0\equiv f(x_{n+1})\equiv a_n\prod_{i=1}^n(x_{n+1}-x_i)\pmod p \]

but the right-hand side is obviously not a multiple of \(p\), so the assumption leads to a contradiction.

Corollary 3

If the congruence equation \(\sum_{i=0}^nb_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\) has more than \(n\) solutions, then

\[ (\forall i=0,1,\dots,n),~~p\mid b_i. \]
Theorem 4

If the number of solutions of equation \((6)\) is not \(p\), then there necessarily exists a polynomial with integer coefficients \(r(x)\) with \(\deg r<p\) such that \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and \(r(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) have the same solution set.

Proof

Without loss of generality let \(n\geq p\), and divide \(f(x)\) with remainder:

\[ f(x)=g(x)\left(x^p-x\right)+r(x) \]

where \(\deg r<p\).

By Fermat's little theorem, for every integer \(x\) we have \(x^p\equiv x\pmod p\), hence

  • if \(r(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\), then by Corollary 2, \(f(x)\) has \(p\) distinct solutions;
  • if \(r(x)\not\equiv 0\pmod p\), then from \(f(x)\equiv r(x)\pmod p\) it follows that \(f(x)\) and \(r(x)\) have the same solution set.

We can use this theorem to reduce the degree of a congruence equation.

Theorem 5

Let \(n\leq p\). The equation

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{7} \]

has \(n\) solutions if and only if there exist polynomials with integer coefficients \(q(x)\), \(r(x)~(\deg r < n)\) such that

\[ x^p-x=f(x)q(x)+pr(x). \tag{8} \]
Proof

  • Necessity: by polynomial division, there exist polynomials with integer coefficients \(q(x)\), \(r_1(x)~(\deg r_1 < n)\) such that

    \[ x^p-x=f(x)q(x)+r_1(x). \]

    If equation \((7)\) has \(n\) solutions, then \(r_1\equiv 0\pmod p\) also has the same \(n\) solutions, and hence by Corollary 3 there exists a polynomial with integer coefficients \(r(x)\) satisfying \(r_1(x)=pr(x)\); this proves the statement.

  • Sufficiency: if \((8)\) holds, then by Fermat's little theorem, for every integer \(x\),

    \[ 0\equiv x^p-x\equiv f(x)q(x)\pmod p. \]

    That is, the equation \(f(x)q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) has \(p\) solutions.

    Let the number of solutions of equation \((7)\) be \(s\); then by Lagrange's theorem \(s\leq n\).

    Moreover, since \(\deg q=p-n\), by Lagrange's theorem the number of solutions of \(q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) does not exceed \(p-n\), and the solution set of the equation \(f(x)q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) is the union of the solution set of \(f(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\) and the solution set of \(q(x)\equiv 0\pmod p\), so \(s+(p-n)\geq p\), i.e. \(s\geq n\).

    Therefore \(s=n\).

For non-monic polynomials, since \(\mathbf{Z}_p\) is a field, they can be converted into monic polynomials, so the theorem applies.

Theorem 6

Let \(n\mid p-1\) and \(p\nmid a\). The equation

\[ x^n\equiv a\pmod p\tag{9} \]

has a solution if and only if

\[ a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p. \]

Moreover, if \((9)\) has a solution, the number of solutions is \(n\).

Note

For the concrete structure of the solution set of equation \((9)\), see k-th power residues.

Proof

  • Necessity: if equation \((9)\) has a solution \(x_0\), then

    \[ a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv {\left(x_0^n\right)}^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p \]

  • Sufficiency: if \(a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}\equiv 1\pmod p\), then

    \[ \begin{aligned} x^p-x&=x\left(x^{p-1}-1\right)\\ &=x\left(\left(x^n\right)^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}+a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-1\right)\\ &=\left(x^n-a\right)P(x)+x\left(a^{\frac{p-1}{n}}-1\right)\\ \end{aligned} \]

    where \(P(x)\) is some polynomial with integer coefficients, so by Theorem 5 equation \((9)\) has \(n\) solutions.

Methods for solving higher-degree congruences (and systems)

First, using the Chinese remainder theorem, we convert solving a system of congruence equations into solving congruence equations, and solving a congruence equation modulo a composite \(m\) into solving congruence equations modulo prime powers. Then, using Theorem 1, we convert solving a congruence equation modulo a prime power into solving a congruence equation modulo a prime.

Combining the theorems on congruence equations modulo a prime, we only need to consider how to solve the equation

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p \]

where \(p\) is a prime and \(n<p\).

By substituting \(x\) with \(x-\dfrac{a_{n-1}}{n}\) we can eliminate the \(x^{n-1}\) term, so we only need to consider how to solve the equation

\[ x^n+\sum_{i=0}^{n-2}a_ix^i\equiv 0\pmod p\tag{10} \]

where \(p\) is a prime and \(n<p\).

  • If \(n=1\), see Linear congruences for the method.
  • If \(n=2\), see Quadratic residues for the method.

  • If equation \((10)\) can be converted into

    \[ x^n\equiv a\pmod p, \]

    see k-th power residues for the method.
