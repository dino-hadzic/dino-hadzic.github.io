Sadržaj

Lema o podizanju eksponenta (Lift the Exponent, LTE) često je korišten teorem elementarne teorije brojeva.

Definirajmo \(\nu_p(n)\) kao eksponent prostog faktora \(p\) u kanonskom rastavu cijelog broja \(n\), tj. \(\nu_p(n)\) zadovoljava \(p^{\nu_p(n)}\mid n\) i \(p^{\nu_p(n)+1}\nmid n\).

Budući da je sadržaj leme o podizanju eksponenta poduži, podijelit ćemo ga u tri dijela:

U nastavku neka je \(p\) prost broj, \(x,y\) cijeli brojevi takvi da \(p\nmid x\) i \(p\nmid y\), a \(n\) pozitivni cijeli broj.

Prvi dio

Za sve proste brojeve \(p\) i cijele brojeve \(n\) takve da je \((n,p)=1\):

  1. Ako \(p\mid x-y\), tada:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y) \]

  2. Ako \(p\mid x+y\), tada za neparan \(n\) vrijedi:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n+y^n\right)=\nu_p(x+y) \]
Dokaz

Ako \(p\mid x-y\), nije teško uočiti da \(p\mid x-y\iff x\equiv y\pmod p\), pa očito vrijedi:

\[ \sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x^iy^{n-1-i}\equiv nx^{n-1}\not\equiv 0\pmod p \]

Nadalje, iz \(x^n-y^n=(x-y)\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x^iy^{n-1-i}\) slijedi tvrdnja.

Za slučaj \(p\mid x+y\) dokaz je sličan.

Drugi dio

Ako je \(p\) neparan prost broj:

  1. Ako \(p\mid x-y\), tada:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(n) \]

  2. Ako \(p\mid x+y\), tada za neparan \(n\) vrijedi:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n+y^n\right)=\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n) \]
Dokaz

Ako \(p\mid x-y\), stavimo \(y=x+kp\); dovoljno je dokazati slučaj \(p\mid n\).

  • Ako je \(n=p\), prema binomnom teoremu:

    \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=0}^{p-1}x^{p-1-i}y^i&=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}x^{p-1-i}\sum_{j=0}^i\binom{i}{j}x^j(kp)^{i-j}\\ &\equiv px^{p-1} \pmod{p^2}\\ \end{aligned} \]

    pa je

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+1 \]

  • Ako je \(n=p^a\), matematičkom indukcijom dobivamo

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+a \]

Time je tvrdnja dokazana.

Za slučaj \(p\mid x+y\) dokaz je sličan.

Treći dio

Ako je \(p=2\) i \(p\mid x-y\):

  1. Za neparan \(n\) vrijedi (isto kao u točki 1 prvog dijela):

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y) \]

  2. Za paran \(n\) vrijedi:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n)-1 \]

Osim toga, za gornje \(x,y,n\) vrijedi:

Ako \(4\mid x-y\), tada:

  • \(\nu_2(x+y)=1\)
  • \(\nu_2\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_2(x-y)+\nu_2(n)\)
Dokaz

Dovoljno je dokazati slučaj kad je \(n\) paran. Budući da sada \(p\nmid \dbinom{p}{2}\), ne možemo dokazivati metodom iz drugog dijela.

Stavimo \(n=2^a b\), gdje je \(a=\nu_p(n)\) i \(2\nmid b\); tada je

\[ \begin{aligned} \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)&=\nu_p\left(x^{2^a}-y^{2^a}\right)\\ &=\nu_p\left((x-y)(x+y)\prod_{i=1}^{a-1}\left(x^{2^i}+y^{2^i}\right)\right) \end{aligned} \]

Uočimo da \(2\mid x-y\implies 4\mid x^2-y^2\), pa je \((\forall i\geq 1),~~x^{2^i}+y^{2^i}\equiv 2\pmod 4\), te gornji izraz postaje:

\[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n)-1 \]

Time je tvrdnja dokazana.

Content

The lifting-the-exponent lemma (Lift the Exponent, LTE) is a frequently used theorem in elementary number theory.

Define \(\nu_p(n)\) as the exponent of the prime factor \(p\) in the canonical factorization of the integer \(n\), i.e. \(\nu_p(n)\) satisfies \(p^{\nu_p(n)}\mid n\) and \(p^{\nu_p(n)+1}\nmid n\).

Since the lifting-the-exponent lemma is rather long, we present it in three parts:

In what follows, let \(p\) be a prime, \(x,y\) integers with \(p\nmid x\) and \(p\nmid y\), and \(n\) a positive integer.

Part one

For all primes \(p\) and integers \(n\) with \((n,p)=1\):

  1. If \(p\mid x-y\), then:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y) \]

  2. If \(p\mid x+y\), then for odd \(n\):

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n+y^n\right)=\nu_p(x+y) \]
Proof

If \(p\mid x-y\), it is easy to see that \(p\mid x-y\iff x\equiv y\pmod p\), so obviously:

\[ \sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x^iy^{n-1-i}\equiv nx^{n-1}\not\equiv 0\pmod p \]

Then the claim follows from \(x^n-y^n=(x-y)\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x^iy^{n-1-i}\).

The proof for the case \(p\mid x+y\) is similar.

Part two

If \(p\) is an odd prime:

  1. If \(p\mid x-y\), then:

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(n) \]

  2. If \(p\mid x+y\), then for odd \(n\):

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n+y^n\right)=\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n) \]
Proof

If \(p\mid x-y\), let \(y=x+kp\); we only need to prove the case \(p\mid n\).

  • If \(n=p\), then by the binomial theorem:

    \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=0}^{p-1}x^{p-1-i}y^i&=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}x^{p-1-i}\sum_{j=0}^i\binom{i}{j}x^j(kp)^{i-j}\\ &\equiv px^{p-1} \pmod{p^2}\\ \end{aligned} \]

    hence

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+1 \]

  • If \(n=p^a\), then by mathematical induction we get

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+a \]

This proves the claim.

The proof for the case \(p\mid x+y\) is similar.

Part three

If \(p=2\) and \(p\mid x-y\):

  1. For odd \(n\) (the same as item 1 of part one):

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y) \]

  2. For even \(n\):

    \[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n)-1 \]

In addition, for the \(x,y,n\) above we have:

If \(4\mid x-y\), then:

  • \(\nu_2(x+y)=1\)
  • \(\nu_2\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_2(x-y)+\nu_2(n)\)
Proof

We only need to prove the case where \(n\) is even. Since now \(p\nmid \dbinom{p}{2}\), we cannot use the method of part two.

Let \(n=2^a b\), where \(a=\nu_p(n)\) and \(2\nmid b\); then

\[ \begin{aligned} \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)&=\nu_p\left(x^{2^a}-y^{2^a}\right)\\ &=\nu_p\left((x-y)(x+y)\prod_{i=1}^{a-1}\left(x^{2^i}+y^{2^i}\right)\right) \end{aligned} \]

Note that \(2\mid x-y\implies 4\mid x^2-y^2\), so \((\forall i\geq 1),~~x^{2^i}+y^{2^i}\equiv 2\pmod 4\), and the expression above becomes:

\[ \nu_p\left(x^n-y^n\right)=\nu_p(x-y)+\nu_p(x+y)+\nu_p(n)-1 \]

This proves the claim.
