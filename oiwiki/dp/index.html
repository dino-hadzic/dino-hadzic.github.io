Ovo poglavlje predstavlja dinamičko programiranje (Dynamic Programming, DP), probleme koje rješava te algoritme i optimizacije koji se na njemu temelje.

Dinamičko programiranje metoda je rješavanja složenih problema rastavljanjem izvornog problema na razmjerno jednostavne potprobleme.

Budući da dinamičko programiranje nije neki konkretan algoritam, nego metoda rješavanja određene vrste problema, pojavljuje se uz najrazličitije strukture podataka, a vrste zadataka povezanih s njim još su raznolikije.

U OI-ju se rekurzivna (rekurentna) rješenja problema koji nisu optimizacijski, npr. prebrojavanja, također često nestandardno nazivaju DP-om, pa ih ovo poglavlje navodi zajedno s ostalima. Dinamičko programiranje doista ima mnogo sličnosti s drugim vrstama rekurentnih relacija; pri učenju obratite pozornost na njihove sličnosti i razlike.

Dynamic programming – Wikipedia

This chapter introduces dynamic programming (DP), the problems it solves, and the algorithms and optimizations designed around it.

Dynamic programming is a method of solving complex problems by breaking the original problem down into relatively simple subproblems.

Since dynamic programming is not a specific algorithm but a method for solving a certain kind of problem, it appears together with all kinds of data structures, and the variety of related problems is even broader.

In OI, recurrence-based solutions of non-optimization problems such as counting are also often, somewhat loosely, called DP, so this chapter lists them together with the rest. In fact, dynamic programming does share many similarities with other kinds of recurrences; while studying, pay attention to their similarities and differences.
