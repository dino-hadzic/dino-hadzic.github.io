Ova stranica navodi neke resurse vezane uz algoritamska natjecanja: platforme za online ocjenjivanje, zbirke zadataka, knjige, alate i drugo.

Platforme za online ocjenjivanje

Platforme za online ocjenjivanje (engl. Online Judging System, skraćeno OJ) obično služe za vježbanje rješavanjem zadataka, sudjelovanje u natjecanjima i njihovo organiziranje te za razmjenu iskustava među korisnicima.

Kineske

  • 51Nod: mnogo matematičkih i logičkih zadataka vrijednih pokušaja.
  • Comet OJ: pokrenut 2018., s ciljem da ljubiteljima algoritama pruži platformu za natjecanje, vježbanje i razmjenu; često organizira kvalitetna natjecanja s originalnim zadacima i ima bogatu zbirku zadataka.
  • HDU Online Judge: pokrenut 2005., sustav za online ocjenjivanje Sveučilišta Hangzhou Dianzi, sadrži zadatke s međusveučilišnih treninga.
  • HydroOJ: pokrenut 2021., službena stranica projekta otvorenog koda Hydro. Korisnici mogu stvarati vlastite domene u kojima su dostupne zbirka zadataka, natjecanja, rasprave i druge mogućnosti glavne stranice.
  • Judge Duck Online: temelji se na projektu otvorenog koda JudgeDuck koji je razvio 松松松; vrijeme izvođenja ocjenjivanih programa mjeri s točnošću od mikrosekunde. (Zadataka je malo.)
  • LibreOJ: pokrenut 2017. Temelji se na projektu otvorenog koda Lyrio; „Libre” znači slobodan. Svi testni podaci zadataka i sav predani kôd otvoreni su svim korisnicima. Trenutačno ga održava Menci.
  • CDOJ: sustav za online ocjenjivanje Sveučilišta elektroničkih znanosti i tehnologije Kine (UESTC), pokrenut 2012.
  • Luogu: pokrenut 2013., ima veliku zajednicu i razmjerno potpune zbirke pravih zadataka i vježbi sa svih vrsta OI natjecanja. Nudi plaćene obrazovne usluge.
  • Nowcoder (牛客网): pokrenut 2014., nudi pripremu za tehničke razgovore za posao, zajednicu, zapošljavanje i druge usluge.
  • OpenJudge: pokrenut 2005., platforma za ocjenjivanje za grupe koju je razvio tim POJ-a.
  • POJ: sustav za online ocjenjivanje Sveučilišta u Pekingu, pokrenut 2003., jedan od OJ-eva s najdužom poviješću u Kini. Sadrži mnogo zadataka na engleskom, od osnovnih do dobrih zadataka vrijednih pokušaja. Zadaci s POJ-a mogu se predavati i u zbirci Bailian.
  • PTA (拼题 A): pokrenut 2016., proizvod tvrtke Hangzhou PAT Education Technology proizašle iz Sveučilišta Zhejiang.
  • QOJ: prikuplja mnogo zadataka s kineskih i stranih OI i ICPC natjecanja; vrijedan za trening.
  • Universal Online Judge: pokrenut 2014.; „Universal” znači opći, projekt je otvorenog koda; OJ čiji je autor VFK: mnogo originalnih natjecateljskih zadataka te zadataka CCF-a/THU-a, veće težine.
  • Vijos: pokrenut 2005. Poslužitelj, ocjenjivač i drugi dijelovi otvorenog su koda.
  • WZOI: pokrenut 2017., sustav za ocjenjivanje otvorenog koda koji održava Srednja škola Wenzhou u pokrajini Zhejiang.
  • ZOJ: sustav za online ocjenjivanje Sveučilišta Zhejiang, pokrenut 2001.

Strane

  • AizuOJ: sustav za online ocjenjivanje japanskog Sveučilišta Aizu, pokrenut 2004. Sadrži zadatke s nekoliko japanskih srednjoškolskih i sveučilišnih natjecanja u programiranju te vlastite uvodne tečajeve programiranja, struktura podataka i algoritama.
  • AtCoder: japanski OJ; u japanskoj inačici održavaju se natjecanja japanskih škola koja se ne prikazuju u engleskoj. Zadaci su zanimljivi i kvalitetni.
  • CodeChef: indijski OJ, redovito održava natjecanja. Sustav se temelji na SPOJ-evu Sphere Engineu.
  • Codeforces: ruski OJ, pokrenut 2010., osnivač je Mike Mirzayanov. Ima više serija natjecanja, a podržava i da pojedinci sastavljaju zadatke i traže organizaciju natjecanja. Zadaci su kvalitetni.
  • CSES (Code Submission Evaluation System): zbirka zadataka podijeljena po temama, s ciljem da postane sveobuhvatna kvalitetna zbirka zadataka; razvija je uglavnom Antti Laaksonen, autor Competitive Programmer's Handbooka, od 2013.
  • CS Academy
  • DMOJ: kanadski OJ otvorenog koda sa širokom podrškom za jezike; zbirka zadataka je arhiva velikih natjecanja, a redovito organizira i vlastita natjecanja.
  • HackerRank: mnogo natjecanja.
  • Kattis: zbirka zadataka sadrži uglavnom zadatke slične onima s ICPC natjecanja; prema riješenim zadacima ocjenjuje razinu korisnika i preporučuje mu zadatke primjerene težine u četiri kategorije: trivial/easy/medium/hard, pri čemu se težina zadataka procjenjuje sustavom sličnim ELO rejtingu.
  • LeetCode: stranica za vježbanje zadataka za programerske razgovore za posao; ima kinesku podružnicu LeetCode China.
  • Light OJ
  • Open Trains: platforma za trening ruskog natjecanja Open Cup, izgrađena na sustavu otvorenog koda ejudge, podržava virtualna natjecanja; zbirka sadrži zadatke s Open Cupa proteklih godina te s kampova u Petrozavodsku.
  • SPOJ: pokrenut 2003.; njegov pozadinski sustav Sphere Engine komercijaliziran je 2008.; podržava ocjenjivanje zadataka „sviđa mi se” i oznake.
  • TopCoder: pokrenut 2001.; njegova zajednica natjecateljskog programiranja ima mnogo natjecanja; glavna mu je djelatnost danas tehnički crowdsourcing.
  • TimusOJ: pokrenut 2000., razvilo ga je Uralsko federalno sveučilište; ima najveću rusku zbirku zadataka za online ocjenjivanje, zadaci su uglavnom sa sveučilišnih natjecanja Uralskog federalnog sveučilišta, Uralskog prvenstva, ICPC regionalnog natjecanja za Ural te s kampova u Petrozavodsku.
  • Online Judge (bivši UVaOJ): pokrenut 1995., OJ koji se prvi proslavio u svijetu; osnivač je profesor Miguel Ángel Revilla sa španjolskog Sveučilišta u Valladolidu (UVa). Budući da je profesor Revilla nažalost preminuo 2018., a Sveučilište u Valladolidu prestalo s održavanjem, UVaOJ je od srpnja 2019. preimenovan u Online Judge. Sadašnji održavatelji platforme grade novu platformu za ocjenjivanje na GitHubu.
  • Yandex: arhiva Sveruske informatičke olimpijade proteklih godina.

This page lists some resources related to algorithm competitions: online judges, problem collections, books, tools and more.

Online judges

Online judges (Online Judging System, OJ for short) are generally used for practice by solving problems, participating in and organizing contests, and exchanging ideas among users.

Chinese

  • 51Nod: many math and puzzle problems worth trying.
  • Comet OJ: started in 2018, aiming to give algorithm enthusiasts a platform for competing, practicing and exchanging ideas; frequently holds high-quality contests with original problems and has a rich problem set.
  • HDU Online Judge: started in 2005, the online judge of Hangzhou Dianzi University, with problems from multi-university training contests.
  • HydroOJ: started in 2021, the official site of the open-source project Hydro. Users can create their own domains, in which the problem set, contests, discussions and other features of the main site are available.
  • Judge Duck Online: based on the open-source project JudgeDuck developed by 松松松; it measures the running time of judged programs to the microsecond. (Few problems.)
  • LibreOJ: started in 2017. Based on the open-source project Lyrio; "Libre" means free. All test data of the problems and all submitted code are open to every user. Currently maintained by Menci.
  • CDOJ: the online judge of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, started in 2012.
  • Luogu: started in 2013, has a large community and fairly complete collections of past problems and exercises from all kinds of OI contests. Offers paid educational services.
  • Nowcoder (牛客网): started in 2014, provides technical interview preparation, community exchange, corporate recruiting and other services.
  • OpenJudge: started in 2005, a group-oriented judging platform developed by the POJ team.
  • POJ: the online judge of Peking University, started in 2003, one of the OJs with the longest history in China. It contains many problems in English, both basic ones and good ones worth trying. POJ problems can also be submitted in the Bailian problem set.
  • PTA (拼题 A): started in 2016, a product of Hangzhou PAT Education Technology, a spin-off of Zhejiang University.
  • QOJ: collects many problems from Chinese and foreign OI and ICPC contests; valuable for training.
  • Universal Online Judge: started in 2014; "Universal" means general-purpose, the project is open source; the OJ of VFK: many original contest problems and CCF/THU problems, of higher difficulty.
  • Vijos: started in 2005. The server, the judge and other components are open source.
  • WZOI: started in 2017, an open-source judging system maintained by Wenzhou High School in Zhejiang Province.
  • ZOJ: the online judge of Zhejiang University, started in 2001.

Foreign

  • AizuOJ: the online judge of the University of Aizu in Japan, started in 2004. It contains problems from several Japanese high school and university programming contests and comes with introductory courses on programming, data structures and algorithms.
  • AtCoder: a Japanese OJ; the Japanese version hosts contests of Japanese schools that are not shown in the English version. The problems are interesting and of high quality.
  • CodeChef: an Indian OJ that holds contests periodically. The system is based on SPOJ's Sphere Engine.
  • Codeforces: a Russian OJ, started in 2010, founded by Mike Mirzayanov. It has several series of contests and supports individuals proposing problems and applying to organize contests. The problems are of high quality.
  • CSES (Code Submission Evaluation System): a problem set organized by topic that aims to be a comprehensive high-quality problem set; developed mainly by Antti Laaksonen, author of the Competitive Programmer's Handbook, since 2013.
  • CS Academy
  • DMOJ: a Canadian open-source OJ with wide language support; the problem set is an archive of major contests, and it also holds its own contests regularly.
  • HackerRank: has many contests.
  • Kattis: the problem set mainly contains ICPC-style problems; it rates users according to the problems they have solved and recommends problems suited to their level in four difficulty classes, trivial/easy/medium/hard, where problem difficulty is estimated with an Elo-like rating system.
  • LeetCode: a site for practicing programming interview problems, with a Chinese branch: LeetCode China.
  • Light OJ
  • Open Trains: the training platform of the Russian Open Cup, built on the open-source system ejudge, supports virtual contests; the problem set contains Open Cup problems of past years and problems from the Petrozavodsk camps.
  • SPOJ: started in 2003; its backend system Sphere Engine was commercialized in 2008; supports liking and tagging problems.
  • TopCoder: started in 2001; its competitive programming community has many contests; its main business is now technical crowdsourcing.
  • TimusOJ: started in 2000, developed by Ural Federal University; it has the largest online judge problem set in Russia, with problems mainly from Ural Federal University contests, the Ural Championship, the ICPC Ural regional and the Petrozavodsk camps.
  • Online Judge (formerly UVaOJ): started in 1995, the earliest internationally famous OJ, founded by Professor Miguel Ángel Revilla of the University of Valladolid (UVa) in Spain. Because Professor Revilla sadly passed away in 2018 and the University of Valladolid stopped maintaining it, UVaOJ was renamed Online Judge in July 2019. The current maintainers of the platform are building a new judging platform on GitHub.
  • Yandex: archives the All-Russian Olympiad in Informatics of recent years.
