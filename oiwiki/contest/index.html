Ovo poglavlje predstavlja znanja izravno vezana uz natjecanja u programiranju: razna natjecanja, formate natjecanja, tipove zadataka te uobičajene zamke i trikove na natjecanju.

Ovdje se nalaze i plan učenja te često korišteni izvori za učenje.

Poglavlje sadrži i dio o sastavljanju zadataka, s uvodom u znanja potrebna za pripremu natjecateljskih zadataka.

This chapter introduces knowledge directly related to programming contests, including the various competitions, contest formats and problem types, as well as common pitfalls and tricks during a contest.

A learning roadmap and commonly used learning resources can also be found in this chapter.

The chapter also has a problemsetting section introducing the knowledge needed to prepare contest problems.
