O natjecanjima

ICPC

ICPC (International Collegiate Programming Contest, međunarodno studentsko natjecanje u programiranju) organizira ICPC Foundation i najutjecajnije je studentsko natjecanje u računarstvu. Budući da ga je ranije sponzorirao ACM, mnogi ga iz navike zovu ACM natjecanje.

ICPC se uglavnom dijeli na regionalna natjecanja (Regionals) i svjetsko finale (World Finals).

Službena stranica: https://icpc.global

CCPC

Službena stranica: https://ccpc.io

Kinesko studentsko natjecanje u programiranju (China Collegiate Programming Contest).

Bitna razlika u odnosu na ICPC jest da mnoga sveučilišta ne pokrivaju troškove sudjelovanja.

Format natjecanja

Obično tročlani tim koristi jedno računalo, a tijekom natjecanja ima više pokušaja slanja. Rješenja se ocjenjuju u stvarnom vremenu i rezultat se odmah vraća; za svako pogrešno slanje dobiva se kazna od 20 minuta, pa što je više pogrešaka, to je kazna veća. Bodovi za zadatak dobivaju se tek kad su svi testni podaci ispravni. Rang-lista se određuje po broju riješenih zadataka, a pri jednakom broju po ukupnom vremenu. Ukupno vrijeme je zbroj vremena po zadacima; vrijeme zadatka je broj minuta od početka natjecanja do rješavanja tog zadatka plus kazne za taj zadatak.

Na nekim natjecanjima vezanima uz ICPC rang-lista se zamrzava sat vremena prije kraja; slanja i poredak nakon zamrzavanja nisu vidljivi drugim natjecateljima.

Na natjecanjima vezanima uz ICPC natjecatelji smiju imati određenu količinu papirnatih materijala.

Osim ICPC-a i CCPC-a isti format koriste i mnoga druga natjecanja, npr. LeetCode tjedna natjecanja i nacionalno natjecanje u programiranju, Nowcoder natjecanja za početnike, trening-natjecanja i izazovi.

Raspored sezone

  • ICPC/CCPC online natjecanja (kraj kolovoza – početak rujna)
  • ICPC/CCPC regionalna natjecanja (kraj rujna – kraj studenoga)
  • ICPC EC Final/CCPC Final (sredina prosinca)
  • ICPC World Finals (travanj – lipanj sljedeće godine)

About the contests

ICPC

ICPC (International Collegiate Programming Contest) is organised by the ICPC Foundation and is the most influential collegiate computing competition. Because ACM used to sponsor it, many people are still in the habit of calling it the ACM contest.

ICPC is mainly divided into Regionals and the World Finals.

Official website: https://icpc.global

CCPC

Official website: https://ccpc.io

The China Collegiate Programming Contest.

A notable difference from ICPC is that many universities do not reimburse participation costs.

Contest format

Usually a team of three shares one machine and has multiple submission attempts during the contest. Submissions are judged in real time and the verdict is returned immediately; each wrong submission incurs a 20-minute penalty, so the more wrong attempts, the larger the penalty. A problem only scores once all test cases are correct. The ranking is determined by the number of solved problems, and ties are broken by total time. The total time is the sum of the times of each problem; the time of a problem is the number of minutes from the contest start until it is solved plus the penalties on that problem.

In some ICPC-related contests the scoreboard is frozen one hour before the end; submissions and ranking changes after the freeze are not visible to other contestants.

In ICPC-related contests, contestants are allowed to bring a certain amount of printed material.

Besides ICPC and CCPC, many other contests use this format, such as LeetCode weekly contests and the national programming contest, Nowcoder beginner rounds, practice rounds and challenge rounds.

Season schedule

  • ICPC/CCPC online contests (late August to early September)
  • ICPC/CCPC regionals (late September to late November)
  • ICPC EC Final/CCPC Final (mid-December)
  • ICPC World Finals (April to June of the following year)

Training guide

Multi-University Training

Training contests held in summer on HDU OJ. They have prizes and high-quality problems, and over the years a rich collection of resources has accumulated.

Keyword to search for on the OJ: Multi-University Training Contest.
