Mnogi pojmovi trodimenzionalne geometrije zajednički su s dvodimenzionalnom geometrijom; probleme u prostoru možemo rješavati istim metodama kao i probleme u ravnini.

Osnovni pojmovi

Točke, vektori i pravci slični su kao u ravninskoj geometriji, pa ih ovdje ne razrađujemo.

Ravnina

Ravninu možemo prikazati jednom točkom \(P_0(x_0,y_0,z_0)\) te ravnine i njezinim vektorom normale (vektorom okomitim na ravninu) \(\boldsymbol{n}\).

Budući da je \(\boldsymbol{n}\) okomit na ravninu, \(\boldsymbol{n}\) je okomit na sve pravce u toj ravnini. Drugim riječima, ako je \(\boldsymbol{n}=(A,B,C)\), tada za sve točke \(P(x,y,z)\) ravnine vrijedi \(\boldsymbol{n} \cdot \overrightarrow{PP_0} = 0\).

Prema definiciji skalarnog produkta, gornji je izraz ekvivalentan s:

\[ A(x-x_0)+B(y-y_0)+C(z-z_0)=0 \]

Nakon sređivanja dobivamo:

\[ Ax+By+Cz-(Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0)=0 \]

Stavimo li \(D=-(Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0)\), izraz postaje \(Ax+By+Cz+D=0\). Taj izraz nazivamo općim oblikom jednadžbe ravnine.

Osnovne operacije

Kutovi između pravaca i ravnina

Pomoću vektora u prostoru kutovi između pravaca i ravnina lako se računaju.

Za dva ukrštena pravca \(a\), \(b\), kroz točku \(P\) u prostoru povučemo \(a' \parallel a\), \(b' \parallel b'\); šiljasti ili pravi kut koji zatvaraju \(a'\) i \(b'\) naziva se kutom između ukrštenih pravaca \(a\) i \(b\).

Za pravac \(a\) i ravninu \(\alpha\), ako \(a\) siječe \(\alpha\) u \(A\), iz točke \(P\) na \(a\) spustimo okomicu na ravninu \(\alpha\) koja je siječe u \(O\); komplement kuta između \(a\) i \(PO\) naziva se kutom između pravca i ravnine. Posebno, ako \(a \parallel \alpha\) ili \(a \subset \alpha\), kut među njima je \(0^\circ\).

Za dvije ravnine \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), kut između njih definira se kao kut između dvaju pravaca \(a,b\) (gdje \(a \subset \alpha\), \(b \subset \beta\)) okomitih na presječnicu \(l\) tih ravnina.

Definicija kuta između dvaju pravaca i nužni i dovoljni uvjeti za njihov odnos

  • Kut između vektora smjera dvaju pravaca naziva se kutom između tih pravaca.

Iz ove tvrdnje dobivamo sljedeće zaključke: za pravce \(l_1, l_2\) s vektorima smjera \(s_1 (m_1, n_1, p_1)\) i \(s_2 (m_2, n_2, p_2)\), ako je \(\varphi\) kut između pravaca, vrijedi \(\cos \varphi = \dfrac{\left | m_1m_2+n_1n_2+p_1p_2 \right |}{\sqrt{m_1^2+n_1^2+p_1^2}\sqrt{m_2^2+n_2^2+p_2^2}}\).

  • \(l_1 \perp l_2 \iff m_1m_2 + n_1n_2 + p_1p_2 = 0\)

  • \(l_1 \parallel l_2 \iff \dfrac{m_1}{m_2} = \dfrac{n_1}{n_2} = \dfrac{p_1}{p_2}\).

Kut između vektora u prostoru i ravnine

Kad pravac nije okomit na ravninu, kut \(\varphi\) (\(\varphi \in [0, \frac{\pi}{2}]\)) između pravca i njegove projekcije na ravninu naziva se kutom između pravca i ravnine.

Neka je vektor smjera pravca \(s(m, n, p)\), a vektor normale ravnine \(f(a, b, c)\); tada vrijede sljedeće tvrdnje:

  • Sinus kuta: \(\sin\varphi = \dfrac{\left | am + bn + cp \right |}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\sqrt{m^2+n^2+p^2}}\)

  • Pravac je paralelan s ravninom \(\iff am+bn+cp = 0\)

  • Pravac je okomit na ravninu \(\iff \dfrac{a}{m} = \dfrac{b}{n} = \dfrac{c}{p}\)

Dovoljno je zajedno riješiti jednadžbu pravca i jednadžbu ravnine.

Teoremi stereometrije

Teorem o tri sinusa

Neka diedar \(M－AB－N\) ima mjeru \(\alpha\), a u ravnini \(M\) neka je zraka \(AC\) koja s bridom \(AB\) zatvara kut \(\beta\), a s ravninom \(N\) kut \(\gamma\). Tada je \(\sin\gamma = \sin\alpha\cdot\sin\beta\).

Teorem o tri kosinusa

Neka je \(O\) točka ravnine, neka je projekcija pravca \(BO\) kroz točku \(B\) izvan ravnine na tu ravninu \(AO\), a \(OC\) pravac u ravnini. Tada kosinusi kutova \(\angle COB, \angle AOC, \angle AOB\) zadovoljavaju: \(\cos\angle BOC=\cos\angle AOB\cdot\cos\angle AOC\) (\(\angle AOC\), \(\angle AOB\) mogu biti samo šiljasti).

Many concepts of three-dimensional geometry are shared with two-dimensional geometry; we can solve three-dimensional problems with the same methods used for two-dimensional ones.

Basic concepts

Points, vectors and lines are similar to their two-dimensional counterparts and are not elaborated here.

Planes

We can represent a plane by a point \(P_0(x_0,y_0,z_0)\) on it and its normal vector (a vector perpendicular to the plane) \(\boldsymbol{n}\).

Since \(\boldsymbol{n}\) is perpendicular to the plane, \(\boldsymbol{n}\) is perpendicular to every line in the plane. In other words, if \(\boldsymbol{n}=(A,B,C)\), then every point \(P(x,y,z)\) of the plane satisfies \(\boldsymbol{n} \cdot \overrightarrow{PP_0} = 0\).

By the definition of the dot product, this is equivalent to:

\[ A(x-x_0)+B(y-y_0)+C(z-z_0)=0 \]

Rearranging gives:

\[ Ax+By+Cz-(Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0)=0 \]

Let \(D=-(Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0)\); the equation becomes \(Ax+By+Cz+D=0\). We call this the general form of the plane equation.

Basic operations

Angles between lines and planes

Using spatial vectors, the angles between lines and planes in space can be computed quickly.

For two skew lines \(a\), \(b\), through a point \(P\) in space draw \(a' \parallel a\), \(b' \parallel b'\); the acute or right angle formed by \(a'\) and \(b'\) is called the angle between the skew lines \(a\) and \(b\).

For a line \(a\) and a plane \(\alpha\), if \(a\) meets \(\alpha\) at \(A\), drop a perpendicular from a point \(P\) on \(a\) to the plane \(\alpha\) meeting it at \(O\); the complement of the angle between \(a\) and \(PO\) is called the angle between the line and the plane. In particular, if \(a \parallel \alpha\) or \(a \subset \alpha\), the angle between them is \(0^\circ\).

For two planes \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), the angle between them is defined as the angle formed by two lines \(a,b\) (with \(a \subset \alpha\), \(b \subset \beta\)) perpendicular to the intersection line \(l\) of the two planes.

Definition of the angle between two lines and criteria for their relationship

  • The angle between the direction vectors of two lines is called the angle between the two lines.

With this statement we obtain the following conclusions: given two lines \(l_1, l_2\) with direction vectors \(s_1 (m_1, n_1, p_1)\) and \(s_2 (m_2, n_2, p_2)\), letting \(\varphi\) be the angle between the lines, we get \(\cos \varphi = \dfrac{\left | m_1m_2+n_1n_2+p_1p_2 \right |}{\sqrt{m_1^2+n_1^2+p_1^2}\sqrt{m_2^2+n_2^2+p_2^2}}\).

  • \(l_1 \perp l_2 \iff m_1m_2 + n_1n_2 + p_1p_2 = 0\)

  • \(l_1 \parallel l_2 \iff \dfrac{m_1}{m_2} = \dfrac{n_1}{n_2} = \dfrac{p_1}{p_2}\).

Angle between a 3D vector and a plane

When a line is not perpendicular to a plane, the angle \(\varphi\) (\(\varphi \in [0, \frac{\pi}{2}]\)) between the line and its projection onto the plane is called the angle between the line and the plane.

Let the direction vector of the line be \(s(m, n, p)\) and the normal vector of the plane be \(f(a, b, c)\); then the following hold:

  • Sine of the angle: \(\sin\varphi = \dfrac{\left | am + bn + cp \right |}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\sqrt{m^2+n^2+p^2}}\)

  • The line is parallel to the plane \(\iff am+bn+cp = 0\)

  • The line is perpendicular to the plane \(\iff \dfrac{a}{m} = \dfrac{b}{n} = \dfrac{c}{p}\)

Theorems of solid geometry

Three-sine theorem

Let the dihedral angle \(M－AB－N\) have measure \(\alpha\), and let \(AC\) be a ray in the plane \(M\) forming an angle \(\beta\) with the edge \(AB\) and an angle \(\gamma\) with the plane \(N\). Then \(\sin\gamma = \sin\alpha\cdot\sin\beta\).

Three-cosine theorem

Let \(O\) be a point of a plane, let the projection onto the plane of the line \(BO\) through a point \(B\) outside the plane be \(AO\), and let \(OC\) be a line in the plane. Then the cosines of the angles \(\angle COB, \angle AOC, \angle AOB\) satisfy: \(\cos\angle BOC=\cos\angle AOB\cdot\cos\angle AOC\) (\(\angle AOC\), \(\angle AOB\) must be acute).
