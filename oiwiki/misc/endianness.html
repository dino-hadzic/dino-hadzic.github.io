Ova stranica kratko predstavlja pojam i vrste redoslijeda bajtova (endianness).

Uvod

Redoslijed bajtova pravilo je pohrane programskih objekata koji se prostiru na više bajtova; opisuje način na koji su bajtovi objekta raspoređeni.

Vrste

Postoje dva redoslijeda bajtova: little endian (mali kraj) i big endian (veliki kraj).

Radi lakšeg objašnjenja, u nastavku kao primjer uzimamo varijablu na adresi 0x100, tipa int, s heksadecimalnom vrijednošću 0x01234567. Pri tome je 0x01 najznačajniji bajt, a 0x67 najmanje značajan bajt.

Little endian

Little endian znači da računalo objekt u memoriji pohranjuje redom od najmanje značajnog bajta do najznačajnijeg bajta.

Gore spomenuta varijabla prikazuje se ovako:

.... 0x100 0x101 0x102 0x103 ....
.... 67 45 23 01 ....

Big endian

Big endian znači da računalo objekt u memoriji pohranjuje redom od najznačajnijeg bajta do najmanje značajnog bajta.

Gore spomenuta varijabla prikazuje se ovako:

.... 0x100 0x101 0x102 0x103 ....
.... 01 23 45 67 ....

Razlika između dvaju redoslijeda

Zapravo ni jedan od tih dvaju redoslijeda bajtova nije bolji ni lošiji od drugoga. Njihova imena, „mali kraj” i „veliki kraj”, potječu iz knjige „Gulliverova putovanja”. U knjizi se dvije stranke u Liliputu neprekidno ratuju jer se ne mogu dogovoriti treba li jaje razbijati s malog ili s velikog kraja. Baš kao i spor o razbijanju jaja, spor o izboru redoslijeda bajtova nije tehničke naravi.

Naravno, nepodudaranje redoslijeda bajtova dovodi do toga da se binarni podaci pri prijenosu između računala različitih vrsta obrnu. Da bi se to izbjeglo, mrežne aplikacije uspostavile su standard koji jamči da se pri slanju koristi dogovoreni mrežni standard, a ne interni prikaz pojedinog računala.

Uobičajeni izbor redoslijeda

  • Little endian: x86, ARM procesori s Androidom, iOS-om i Windowsima

  • Big endian: Sun, PPC Mac, internet

This page briefly introduces the concept and the kinds of byte order (endianness).

Introduction

Byte order is the storage rule for program objects that span multiple bytes; it describes how the bytes of an object are arranged.

Kinds

There are two byte orders: little endian and big endian.

For convenience, in the following we take as an example a variable located at 0x100, of type int, with hexadecimal value 0x01234567. Here 0x01 is the most significant byte and 0x67 is the least significant byte.

Little endian

Little endian means that the machine stores an object in memory in order from the least significant byte to the most significant byte.

The variable mentioned above is represented as follows:

.... 0x100 0x101 0x102 0x103 ....
.... 67 45 23 01 ....

Big endian

Big endian means that the machine stores an object in memory in order from the most significant byte to the least significant byte.

The variable mentioned above is represented as follows:

.... 0x100 0x101 0x102 0x103 ....
.... 01 23 45 67 ....

Difference between the two orders

In fact, neither of these two byte orders is better or worse than the other. Their names, "little endian" and "big endian", come from the book "Gulliver's Travels". In the book, two factions in Lilliput fight endlessly because they cannot agree on whether an egg should be cracked from the little end or the big end. Just like the argument about cracking eggs, the argument about which byte order to choose is non-technical.

Of course, inconsistent byte order causes binary data to be reversed when transferred between machines of different types. To avoid this, network applications have established a standard guaranteeing that the agreed network standard is used during transmission, rather than the internal representation of a particular machine.

Conventional choices of order

  • Little endian: x86, ARM processors running Android, iOS, and Windows

  • Big endian: Sun, PPC Mac, Internet
