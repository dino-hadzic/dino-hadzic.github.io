Imate \(n\) poslova i trebate pronaći redoslijed njihova izvršavanja s najmanjim troškom. Izvršavanje \(i\)-tog posla traje \(t_i\) vremena, a čekanje \(i\)-tog posla u trajanju \(t\) stoji \(f_i(t)\).

Formalno: zadano je \(n\) funkcija \(f_i\) i \(n\) brojeva \(t_i\); treba pronaći permutaciju \(p\) koja minimizira

\[ F(p)=\sum_{i=1}^nf_{p_i}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p_j}\right) \]

Posebne funkcije troška

Linearna funkcija troška

Najprije razmotrimo slučaj u kojem su sve funkcije linearne, tj. \(f_i(x)=c_ix+d_i\), gdje su \(c_i\) nenegativni cijeli brojevi. Očito možemo unaprijed zbrojiti slobodne članove, pa funkcije poprimaju oblik \(f_i(x)=c_ix\).

Promotrimo dvije permutacije \(p\) i \(p'\), gdje je \(p'\) permutacija dobivena iz \(p\) zamjenom elemenata na položajima \(i\) i \(i+1\). Tada je

\[ \begin{aligned} F(p')-F(p)&=c_{p'_i}\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p'_j}+c_{p'_{i+1}}\sum_{j=1}^{i}t_{p'_j} -\left(c_{p_i}\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p_j}+c_{p_{i+1}}\sum_{j=1}^{i}t_{p_j}\right)\\ &=c_{p_i}t_{p_{i+1}}-c_{p_{i+1}}t_{p_i} \end{aligned} \]

Dakle, dovoljno je sortirati strategijom „zamijeni ako je \(c_{p_i}t_{p_{i+1}}-c_{p_{i+1}}t_{p_i}<0\)”. Zapišemo li to u obliku \(\dfrac{c_{p_i}}{t_{p_i}}<\dfrac{c_{p_{i+1}}}{t_{p_{i+1}}}\), možemo to shvatiti kao sortiranje permutacije silazno po \(\dfrac{c_i}{t_i}\).

Pri rješavanju ovog problema ideja nam je promotriti što se mijenja nakon male perturbacije i pohlepno odabrati optimalno rješenje.

Eksponencijalna funkcija troška

Razmotrimo funkciju troška oblika \(f_i(x)=c_i\mathrm{e}^{ax}\), gdje je \(c_i\ge 0,a>0\).

Slijedimo prethodnu ideju i promatramo promjenu troška izazvanu zamjenom elemenata na položajima \(i\) i \(i+1\). Konačni algoritam jest sortiranje permutacije uzlazno po \(\dfrac{1-\mathrm{e}^{at_i}}{c_i}\).

Jednaka monotono rastuća funkcija

Razmotrimo slučaj u kojem su sve \(f_i(x)\) ista monotono rastuća funkcija. Tada očito samo trebamo sortirati permutaciju uzlazno po \(t_i\).

Livshits–Kladovljev teorem

Livshits–Kladovljev teorem vrijedi ako i samo ako je funkcija troška jedan od sljedećih triju slučajeva:

  • linearna funkcija: \(f_i(t) = c_it + d_i\), gdje je \(c_i\ge 0\);
  • eksponencijalna funkcija: \(f_i(t) = c_i \mathrm{e}^{a t} + d_i\), gdje je \(c_i,a>0\);
  • jednaka monotono rastuća funkcija: \(f_i(t) = \phi(t)\), gdje je \(\phi(t)\) monotono rastuća funkcija.

Teorem je dokazan uz pretpostavku da je funkcija troška dovoljno glatka (postoji treća derivacija). U ta tri slučaja optimalno se rješenje problema može pronaći jednostavnim sortiranjem u vremenu \(O(n\log n)\).

You have \(n\) jobs and need to find the order of executing them with the minimum cost. The \(i\)-th job takes \(t_i\) time to execute, and making the \(i\)-th job wait for time \(t\) costs \(f_i(t)\).

Formally: given \(n\) functions \(f_i\) and \(n\) numbers \(t_i\), find a permutation \(p\) that minimizes

\[ F(p)=\sum_{i=1}^nf_{p_i}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p_j}\right) \]

Special cost functions

Linear cost function

First we consider the case where all functions are linear, i.e. \(f_i(x)=c_ix+d_i\), where the \(c_i\) are non-negative integers. Obviously we can add up the constant terms in advance, so the functions take the form \(f_i(x)=c_ix\).

Consider two permutations \(p\) and \(p'\), where \(p'\) is obtained from \(p\) by swapping the elements at positions \(i\) and \(i+1\). Then

\[ \begin{aligned} F(p')-F(p)&=c_{p'_i}\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p'_j}+c_{p'_{i+1}}\sum_{j=1}^{i}t_{p'_j} -\left(c_{p_i}\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}t_{p_j}+c_{p_{i+1}}\sum_{j=1}^{i}t_{p_j}\right)\\ &=c_{p_i}t_{p_{i+1}}-c_{p_{i+1}}t_{p_i} \end{aligned} \]

So it suffices to sort with the strategy "swap if \(c_{p_i}t_{p_{i+1}}-c_{p_{i+1}}t_{p_i}<0\)". Written in the form \(\dfrac{c_{p_i}}{t_{p_i}}<\dfrac{c_{p_{i+1}}}{t_{p_{i+1}}}\), this can be understood as sorting the permutation in decreasing order of \(\dfrac{c_i}{t_i}\).

To handle this problem, our idea is to consider what changes after a small perturbation and greedily choose the optimal solution.

Exponential cost function

Consider a cost function of the form \(f_i(x)=c_i\mathrm{e}^{ax}\), where \(c_i\ge 0,a>0\).

We follow the previous idea and consider the change in cost caused by swapping the elements at positions \(i\) and \(i+1\). The resulting algorithm is to sort the permutation in increasing order of \(\dfrac{1-\mathrm{e}^{at_i}}{c_i}\).

Identical monotonically increasing function

Consider the case where all \(f_i(x)\) are the same monotonically increasing function. Then obviously we just sort the permutation in increasing order of \(t_i\).

Livshits–Kladov theorem

The Livshits–Kladov theorem holds if and only if the cost function is one of the following three cases:

  • linear function: \(f_i(t) = c_it + d_i\), where \(c_i\ge 0\);
  • exponential function: \(f_i(t) = c_i \mathrm{e}^{a t} + d_i\), where \(c_i,a>0\);
  • identical monotonically increasing function: \(f_i(t) = \phi(t)\), where \(\phi(t)\) is a monotonically increasing function.

The theorem is proven under the assumption that the cost function is sufficiently smooth (the third derivative exists). In these three cases, the optimal solution of the problem can be found by simple sorting in \(O(n\log n)\) time.
